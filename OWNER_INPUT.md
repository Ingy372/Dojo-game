# What the owner can tell the build agent

You don't need to know how OT servers are built to make them better. Your experience as a player is the input nobody else has. Answer in your own words, in any order, directly in this file (or open a GitHub issue with the "Owner note" template). Short answers are fine. Skip anything you don't care about.

## 1. Servers you've played
- Which OT servers did you play the longest? Version (7.4, 8.6, 10.98, custom)?
- What kept you there? What made you quit?
- Any server whose launch week felt great? What happened that week?

## 2. Hunting and leveling
- Your favorite hunting spot ever, and why (layout, loot, danger, people)?
- Solo or team hunts? How big were your usual teams?
- Moments that felt like a grind you hated. Moments that felt like a grind you loved.

## 3. PvP
- How did PvP actually play out on the servers you liked? Guild wars, PKs on the road, boss fights?
- What made PvP feel fair or unfair? Any skull rules you liked or hated?
- How did people treat new players?

## 4. Paths and combat
- Which vocation did you play most, and what made it fun to press buttons?
- Anything about Tibia combat you'd change for Spirebound (speed, healing, spell feel)?

## 5. Economy and loot
- What did players actually trade? What was always worthless?
- Did you ever see a server's economy break? What happened?
- How did people feel about houses?

## 6. Bosses and the race
- A boss fight you remember well — what made it memorable?
- How did guilds organize for big bosses (Discord, voice, sign-up sheets)?

## 7. Secrets and exploration
- Any hidden place or quest you loved discovering?
- Would you share a secret with your team, or sell it? Be honest — that's the design question.

## 8. Theme and names
- Any original names, places, or story ideas you want in the game?
- Floors, monsters, or set pieces from the source you want echoed (structure only, original names)?

## 9. Practical
- Target launch window and how much time per week you can play-test.
- Monthly hosting budget you're comfortable with.
- Will anyone help you (GM, moderator, artist, friend testers)?
- Your preferred time zone for launch and events (default: US Eastern).

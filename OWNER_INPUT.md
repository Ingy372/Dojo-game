## 2. Hunting and leveling

- Your favorite hunting spot ever, and why (layout, loot, danger, people)? the hydras in the server cyntara. they were a monster healed and was usually to tough to take at level 400 (when unlocked) and that provided better xp way into the higher levels than even the content that was meant for your level. due to it being good xp, it made for a lot of fun fights. the spawn was laid out where it started with groups of 2-3 hydra in the beginning, then going up a floor there was a room packed with them, if you could clear that, there was a semi hidden rope spot that lead to a big spawn with decent groupings. if you explored enough, there was even another dungeon hidden inside the hydra spawn that offered monster with slightly less xp than hydras, but a lot easier to kill and a good spawn of them. 
- Solo or team hunts? How big were your usual teams? i usually did solo hunts, i dont feel like many servers got the team hunt algorithm right to make it worth the time.

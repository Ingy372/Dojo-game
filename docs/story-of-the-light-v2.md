# Dojo Ascent: The Story of the Light (version 2, patched draft)

Nov 25, 2026 · **Approved by Jay, Nov 25.** Not built into the game yet; it goes in one piece at a time, as listed at the end of version 1.

This is the Nov 25 story with its holes patched and its thin spots filled. The heart, the Gospel order, the chapters and the ending are unchanged. Everything added or changed is marked **[New]** or **[Changed]**, and the reasons are in "What was patched and why" at the bottom.

Verses and covenants appear only exactly as written on the dojo walls (`docs/faith-and-creed.md`). Every line a character says is a draft for Jay to approve.

## The heart of the story

The story is about grace: a lost boy who took the light for himself is loved, forgiven and brought home, and the player learns the Gospel by living it alongside him.

- **The light** stands for God's truth and love. It is never "magic power".
- **The Shadows** are not creatures with a will. They are what spreads where the light is missing.
- **The lost student** is not a villain. He is the one sheep the shepherd goes looking for.
- **The hero wins by loving, not by beating him.** Fights are against the darkness, never against a person.
- **Two layers, one story.** **[New]** A six-year-old follows the pictures: a sad boy, a kind friend, a town getting brighter. A thirteen-year-old follows three mysteries that run under the pictures (see "The three mysteries").

## The world

**The Tower and the light.** A light burned at the top of the Tower and shone over the town. The dojo taught that the light was not theirs: it was a gift, and their job was to carry it and share it.

**[Changed] When it broke.** The light broke **last winter**, not "long ago". Everyone in town remembers the night. That is why Eli is still a boy, why his class photo is still on the wall, and why people are still hurt.

**[New] How it broke into pieces.** The light broke into three kinds of pieces, and the whole story runs on them:

| Piece | Where it is | Who can bring it home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sparks** (tiny) | Inside every Shadow | Anyone. They fly home when a Shadow bursts. |
| **Nine great pieces** | One under each Guardian, one per belt | Nobody alone. This is why Eli has been stuck. |
| **The Heart** (the biggest) | At the top, where it broke | Nobody at all. It can only be given back. |

- The hero's own spark (the one that lands in their hands in the opening) is not one of the nine. It is what lets the hero set light free, and nobody ever takes it away.
- Eli can only gather sparks and small chips. He has never beaten a Guardian, because he fights alone. The only great piece he ever holds is the one he snatches in Chapter 1, after the *hero* beats the Floor Keeper.
- **The town lamp.** In the town square stands a great lamp with nine empty places around a hollow centre. Each great piece brought home lights one place. Players can always see how far the story has come by looking at the lamp.

**The town.** The town square, the Action Zone dojo, Miss Hannah's pro shop, Grandmaster Michael's Street Smarts House and every student's Home Dojo. Since the light broke the town is dim and grey. Each piece brought home makes it brighter, and shops and people come back.

**What the Shadows are.** Simply what fills a place when the light is gone.

- A Shadow takes the shape of whatever it sits beside.
- Shadows never think or plan.
- **[Changed] Shadows have no words of their own.** A few can only *echo* words that were once said near them, like a cave echoes. (This is what lets the Echo Mask and the final Guardian "speak" without breaking the rule.)
- When the hero defeats one, it bursts into sparks. Every fight is light being set free.
- In the Home Dojo, Shadows are faceless grey wisps in the corners that fade when the room is swept and the lamp is lit.

**[Changed] The Guardians are never people.** When the light went out, the darkness settled over the places where the town's good work was done, and took the shape of the worker: the baker's oven, the gardener's statues, the ferryman's dock. The real baker is safe in town, sad, with a cold oven. Beating the Hearth Cook is not fighting the baker; it is getting her bakery back. When a Guardian falls, the darkness cracks off, the great piece under it is freed, and that person's shop in town reopens.

**The Gates.** A Gate seals the top of every belt's floors. It opens only when the student earns their next belt in real class. The story's biggest moment for each belt plays after the real promotion celebration, never instead of it and never bigger than it.

**[New] Belts are earned; the light is a gift.** Older students will notice that the game says "you can't earn the light" while Gates only open for earned belts. The story says so out loud, at Green belt. Sensei: "A belt is earned. It tells the truth about your training. But nobody trains their way into being loved. That part was always a gift."

**[New] Students who start at a higher belt.** A student who joins the game as, say, a Blue belt still starts the story at Chapter 1. Their Gates are already open, so the story plays as fast as they climb. Nobody skips the story and nobody is held back by it.

## The characters

**The hero (the player).** A new student who did nothing special to deserve the light. A spark simply lands in their hands. **[New]** The hero has their own stumble at Yellow belt (pride), so they are a fellow traveller, not Eli's rescuer.

**Sensei.** Warm, steady and honest. **[New] Why Sensei hasn't already brought Eli home:** Sensei has climbed after him many times. Eli runs every time, because Sensei is the person he most let down. Sensei: "I can clear a floor. I can't make him come home." Eli doesn't run from the hero, because the hero is new and doesn't know what he did. Sensei has kept Eli's name peg on the dojo wall, empty and dusted, all winter.

**Eli, the lost student** (draft name).

- **[New] Age and voice:** about 13. The older kid the younger ones looked up to. As he thaws, he is dry and a little funny ("You bow too low. You look like a folding chair."). Older players should like him.
- **Who he was:** Sensei's most gifted student, the Lightbearer, **[New]** one step from black belt.
- **[Changed] What he did:** on festival night the light was to be carried up the Tower by the whole dojo together. Eli slipped out the night before to carry it alone and prove he needed no one. At the last Gate, it broke in his hands.
- **[New] His belt:** frayed and faded so grey that nobody can tell what colour it was. (This is why Gates still open for him: he earned those belts once. It is also why tying on a fresh white belt at Brown belt means so much.)
- **[New] Why he's stuck:** he can pass every Gate except the last, and he cannot beat a single Guardian alone. He has spent the winter gathering sparks and gluing chips together. They always crack.
- **[New] He is turning grey.** The grey on his cloak slowly spreads the longer he stays in the dark. No one says he's in danger. Players just see it, and want to hurry. (It washes off at Red belt.)
- **How he looks:** a normal kid in a faded gi and a grey hooded cloak. No mask, no glowing eyes. Kids should feel sorry for him, not afraid.
- **He is never fought.**

**[New] Mara, Eli's old training partner** (draft name; about 13). She replaces the nameless "grumbling townsperson". She and Eli were supposed to carry the light up side by side on festival night. He left without her. She is the angriest person in town, and it is because she misses him. She now helps Miss Hannah at the pro shop and trains alone. Her forgiving Eli (Red/Black belt) is the town's forgiveness with a face on it.

**The town.** Miss Hannah, Grandmaster Michael, the baker and the other shopkeepers, classmates as echoes.

**The Guardians, one per belt:** Floor Keeper (White), not built (Yellow), Hearth Cook (Orange), Stone Giant (Green), River Watcher (Blue), Ember Knight (Red), Echo Mask (Red/Black), Root Keeper (Brown), Storm Herald (Brown/Black), Lost Lightbearer (Black).

**[Changed] The Lost Lightbearer** is not Eli. A Shadow takes the shape of what it sits beside, and the biggest Shadow in the Tower has sat beside Eli all winter. It wears his old shape: hood, lantern staff, proud walk. It has no mind. It only echoes what he used to say.

## The three mysteries [New]

These are what make an older student want the next chapter. Younger kids don't need them; the pictures carry the story.

1. **What broke the light?** Hinted by Eli's notes from Chapter 2 on. Answered at Orange belt.
2. **What is Eli still hiding?** At Orange he admits *what* he did. Players can tell there's more. Answered at Blue belt: the light was meant to be carried together, he left Mara behind, and he lied to Sensei that night.
3. **Who is the second hooded figure?** From Chapter 3, players sometimes glimpse a hooded shape that is *not* Eli: too tall, too still, standing where Eli couldn't be. Eli has seen it too and won't talk about it. Answered at Brown/Black: it is the Shadow wearing his old shape, and it is waiting at the top.

**[New] Eli's notes (the thread between big scenes).** Real belts take months, so three scenes per belt is too thin on its own. Scattered through the floors are torn pages from Eli's training notebook, about three per belt, one or two sentences each, kept in the Book. Early pages are proud ("Be the best. Need no one."). Later pages are lonely, then honest. From Yellow belt on, some pages are new, written to the hero ("Thanks for the rice ball. Too much salt."). They are found by playing, never by a timer, and missing days loses nothing.

## The tutorial: Chapters 1 to 6

Six short chapters, one after each Tower climb, all inside the White belt floors.

### Chapter 1: The Broken Light (first launch)

*Teaches:* moving, attacking, the Counter, Focus, the first Strike, the first Guardian, bowing in, the Training Station.

- **[Changed] Opening scene.** Narration over the Tower at night: "God gave this town a light." It shines. "Last winter, it broke." It cracks, and pieces fall like slow stars into the Tower. One spark drifts down into the hands of a new student. Sensei: "The light found you. It wasn't luck."
- **The tutorial floor,** as it plays now. Shadows burst into sparks.
- **The Floor Keeper.** The dark cracks off and a great piece floats out.
- **The hook.** A hooded boy drops down, grabs the piece and says: "I have to fix this myself." He runs up the stairs.
- **Sensei, quietly:** "That was Eli. He was my student." Then: "We're going to bring him home."
- **Home.** Sensei shows the Home Dojo and the Training Station.

### Chapter 2: Home, Sweet Dojo (after climb 1)

*Teaches:* sweeping, chores, decorating, Look, the Training Board, Gear, Wear best, the Book.

- **Opening.** A dim, dusty dojo, lamp out, wisps in the corners. Sensei: "When the light is missing, a room gets dark fast."
- **Sweeping, decorating, Look, Training Board,** as now.
- **Ending.** Sensei lights the lamp. "Even a small light pushes back the dark." The class photo: Eli grinning in the front row, **[New]** a girl beside him with her arm around his shoulder (Mara, not named yet). Sensei: "He used to sweep this floor too."
- **[New]** Behind the photo frame the hero finds the first torn notebook page: "Be the best." It goes in the Book. This teaches the Book and starts the notes. **Chapter 2 complete** banner.

### Chapter 3: Tools of the Trade (after climb 2)

*Teaches:* the Tool Belt, pockets, Sensei's Pick, the Fighting Style screen, the KATA button.

- **Opening and practice,** as written.
- **Ending.** In the Tower the hero spots Eli on a high ledge doing the same kata, perfectly. He sees the hero and disappears. Sensei: "He still remembers his forms. He hasn't forgotten who he is."
- **[New] Last picture.** As the hero turns to go, there is a hooded shape on a ledge on the *other* side of the room. It is too tall. It doesn't move. Then it isn't there. Sensei doesn't see it. (Mystery 3 begins. Not scary: a still shape, one second, no sound.)

### Chapter 4: The Real Dojo (after climb 3)

*Teaches:* the town walk, the Action Zone dojo, Miss Hannah's pro shop, kata practice.

- **[New]** The hero sees the town lamp in the square: nine dark places.
- **The pro shop.** Miss Hannah: "Eli bought his first belt here. He was so proud of it." **[Changed]** The girl from the photo is stacking boxes. Mara: "He broke the light. He doesn't get to come back." She leaves.
- **[New]** In the dojo, a row of name pegs. One is empty and has no dust on it.
- **Ending.** Sensei: "No one is too far gone to come home."

### Chapter 5: The Path Up (after climb 4)

*Teaches:* My Path, Missions, "Ready to show Sensei", the Class Chest and Dojo Blessing, Training Points, stripes and Virtues, the Gate.

- **Opening.** The hero finds the White Gate. Eli sits on the other side, his grey, frayed belt in his hands. **[Changed]** Eli: "Gates open when you've earned it. Trust me. I've earned a lot of things." (Sad, not boastful. It tells older players he was high-ranked, and that it didn't save him.)
- **Sensei explains the Gate:** it opens when the hero earns their next belt in real class, never from the game.
- **The real question.** Sensei: "He took the light from you. Can you still love him?" Sensei shows the White belt covenant on the wall, exactly as written: "No matter what you say or do to me, I will always love and pray for you."
- **Then the systems.**

### Chapter 6: Friends in Town (after climb 5)

*Teaches:* the Street Smarts House and Grandmaster Michael, Say phrases, classmate cards, parties, the Monster Journal, "What's ahead".

- **Opening and systems tour,** as written.
- **Ending.** The hero leaves a rice ball and a note, "Come home", on the Gate step. Next time the gift is gone. **[New]** In its place is the rice ball's leaf wrapper, folded into a small paper crane. He isn't ready to talk. He did say thank you. Sensei: "Every floor brings the light back. And every kindness brings him closer."

## The main story, belt by belt

Each part plays at three points: arrival (after the real promotion celebration), the middle floor, and the Guardian and Gate. Notebook pages fill the floors between.

### Part 1: White belt, Love (toward Yellow)

*Verse:* John 3:16. *Covenant:* Unconditional Love. *Life word:* F, Forgiveness.

- **Arrival:** the tutorial.
- **Middle:** scattered notebook pages: "Be the best. Need no one."
- **Guardian and Gate:** the student earns Yellow in real class and the Gate opens. Eli steps back with his fists up. The hero bows: "I forgive you." Eli is stunned and runs, but leaves the great piece from Chapter 1 on the step. **[New]** The first place on the town lamp lights. Mara watches it from the shop door and goes back inside.
- **What kids learn:** God loves us before we do anything right, so we can love people who hurt us.

### Part 2: Yellow belt, Nobody Is Perfect (toward Orange)

*Verse:* Romans 3:10. *Covenant:* Availability. *Life word:* A, Acceptance.

- **Arrival:** a townsperson: "You're a hero! Not like that Eli."
- **[Changed] Middle:** a floor of deep dark that cannot be crossed alone. Sensei offers to walk it with the hero. The scene has the hero wave it off ("I've got this") and the floor gives way; every player falls here, so no player is blamed for playing badly. A hand pulls the hero up. It's Eli. "You sound like me," he says, and he's gone. Sensei, with the verse exactly as on the wall: "There is no one righteous, no not one." Then: "Not Eli. Not you. Not me." (Nobody's echo gets hurt, and it is the first time Eli helps.)
- **Guardian and Gate:** Eli is hungry and cold near the Gate. The hero shares their own food and blanket: Availability lived out. Eli accepts, barely. Second place lit. **[New]** First notebook page written *to* the hero.
- **What kids learn:** we all need grace, so we don't look down on anyone.

### Part 3: Orange belt, The Truth Comes Out (toward Green)

*Verse:* Romans 3:23. *Covenant:* Prayer. *Life word:* I, Integrity.

- **Arrival:** the Hearth Cook's kitchen floors. **[Changed]** In town, the baker stands by a cold oven. The darkness has her kitchen, wearing her shape.
- **Middle:** mystery 1 answered. A memory scene: Eli as the Lightbearer, climbing alone at night with the light. It cracks in his hands. He runs.
- **Guardian and Gate:** Eli: "I broke it. It was me." The hero sees Sensei pray for Eli, by name. Sensei: "All of us have fallen short. That's why we pray." Third place lit; the bakery reopens. **[New]** As he leaves, Eli starts to say something more, and stops. (Mystery 2.)
- **What kids learn:** telling the truth about what we did wrong is where change starts.

### Part 4: Green belt, You Can't Earn It (toward Blue)

*Verse:* Romans 6:23. *Covenant:* Openness. *Life word:* T, Trust.

- **Arrival:** the Stone Garden floors. The Stone Giant is a picture of a heart turned to stone.
- **Middle:** Eli's hideout: hundreds of sparks and chips glued into a lumpy, flickering ball. It cracks while the hero watches. "If I fix it, they'll have to take me back."
- **[Changed] Guardian and Gate:** for the first time, Eli doesn't run when Sensei arrives. Sensei: "You can't earn the light. It was always a gift." Then the belts line: "A belt is earned... nobody trains their way into being loved." Eli opens up about how alone he has been (Openness). **[New]** Sensei tells him his peg is still on the wall. Fourth place lit.
- **What kids and adults learn:** salvation is a gift, not a reward for being good enough. The theological heart of the story.

### Part 5: Blue belt, Love That Goes First (toward Red)

*Verse:* Romans 5:8. *Covenant:* Honest. *Life word:* H, Honor.

- **Arrival:** the River floors. Eli follows at a distance, helping in small ways. **[New]** His cloak is greyer than before.
- **Middle:** a flood of darkness rushes at Eli. Sensei steps in front and takes it. Sensei is hurt but recovers. Eli: "Why would you do that? I don't deserve it." Sensei: "That's what love does. Jesus did it for me first."
- **[Changed] Guardian and Gate:** mystery 2 answered. This must be different from the Orange confession, so it is the part he left out. Sitting by Sensei while Sensei rests, Eli tells all of it: the light was meant to be carried together; he lied to Sensei that night; and he left Mara waiting at the Tower door until morning. "Breaking it was an accident. Leaving her wasn't." Fifth place lit.
- **What kids learn:** Jesus loved us while we were still getting it wrong.

### Part 6: Red belt, Coming Clean (toward Red/Black)

*Verse:* 1 John 1:9. *Covenant:* Sensitivity. *Life word:* D, Discipline.

- **Arrival:** the Ember floors. Eli wants to come home but is afraid of the town.
- **Middle:** the hero helps him get ready, gently (Sensitivity). **[New]** Players pick kind things to say from big buttons; there is no wrong answer, only "not yet" and "ready".
- **[Changed] Guardian and Gate:** in the town square Eli confesses: "I broke the light. I'm sorry." It begins to rain, warm and bright, and the grey runs out of his cloak. He lowers his hood. Most of the town comes forward. **Mara does not.** She looks at him for a long moment and walks away. Sixth place lit. (Forgiveness from God is complete here. Forgiveness from a hurt friend takes one more belt, which is honest, and gives older players a reason to come back.)
- **What kids learn:** when we confess, God forgives and makes us clean.

### Part 7: Red/Black belt, Not Condemned (toward Brown)

*Verse:* John 3:17. *Covenant:* Confidentiality. *Life word:* O, Obedience.

- **Arrival:** the Echo Mask's floors. It echoes every cruel thing ever said about Eli: "You'll never change." **[New]** Some of the echoes are in Mara's voice. She hears them too, and hates how they sound.
- **[Changed] Middle:** in the group (Sensei, the hero, Eli), Eli shares what scares him most: "What if I break it again?" Later a townsperson asks the hero what Eli said. The hero keeps it in the group (Confidentiality, as the wall words it). **Safety line, so this never clashes with Street Smarts.** Sensei: "Keeping a friend's trust is good. But if a secret could hurt someone, you take it to a grown-up you trust. Eli told his in front of me."
- **[Changed] Guardian and Gate:** Mara joins the Echo Mask fight uninvited. When it falls, the voices stop. Then, back in the town square (the same place she walked away last belt), in front of everyone, Mara: "I waited all night for you." Eli: "I know. I'm sorry." She puts out her hand; he takes it. The square cheers. Seventh place lit. *(Jay, Nov 25: her forgiveness happens in the town square.)*
- **What kids learn:** God's Son came to save, not to condemn, so we don't keep reminding people of their worst day.

### Part 8: Brown belt, Starting Over (toward Brown/Black)

*Verse:* John 3:3. *Covenant:* Accountability. *Life word:* E, Empathy.

- **Arrival:** Eli walks into the Action Zone dojo, hangs his grey, frayed belt **[New]** on his old peg, and ties on a brand-new white belt. Born again. A six-year-old white belt looks up at him: "You're new too?" Eli: "Yeah. I'm new."
- **Middle:** the hero and Eli are training partners and keep each other accountable. **[New]** The hero now outranks him, and he bows first. When the hero bows back, Eli grins: "You bow too low. You look like a folding chair." (The first time he jokes. It comes back on his last notebook page.)
- **[Changed] Guardian and Gate:** in the Root Keeper's library floors the hero finds the rest of Eli's notebook: a list, in his handwriting, of everything he ever did wrong. When the Root Keeper falls, the pages lift off, turn to light and are gone. The last notebook page in the Book is blank. Eighth place lit.
- **What kids learn:** in Jesus, anyone can have a brand-new start.

### Part 9: Brown/Black belt, The Wind (toward Black)

*Verse:* John 3:5–8. *Covenant:* none on the wall. *Life word:* S, Servanthood.

- **Arrival:** the Storm floors. Sensei uses the verse to talk about the Holy Spirit: you can't see the wind, but you can see what it does.
- **Middle:** Eli, a white belt again, serves without being asked: carrying supplies, helping younger students. **[New]** Sensei, watching him: "You can't see what changed inside Eli. But everybody can see him carrying the baker's flour."
- **[Changed] Guardian and Gate:** the last Gate, where the light broke, the one Eli never passed. Ninth place lit. Through the bars stands the second hooded figure, clear at last, holding a lantern staff. Mystery 3 answered. Eli: "That's who I was." He grips the bars; the old Eli would charge in alone. Then he lets go and sits down on the step. "I'll wait for you this time." (The real months until Black belt become part of the story: Eli is waiting at the Gate, with the hero, on purpose.)
- **What kids learn:** a changed heart shows up as serving others.

## The ending: Black belt

*Verse:* John 6:40. *No covenant or life word* on the wall. The FAITH DOES letters are complete.

- **Arrival:** the Gate opens. **[New]** Mara, Sensei and the town climb behind them as far as the last stair: this time the light is carried together, the way it was always meant to be.
- **The final Guardian, in three forms** (art already made):
  1. **The hooded Lightbearer:** the Shadow in Eli's old shape. It echoes: "Be the best. Need no one."
  2. **The Shadow Colossus:** all the darkness that spread from that pride.
  3. **The Eclipse Heart:** a dark shell with the Heart of the light inside.
- **[Changed] How it ends:** the hero and Eli fight together. When the Eclipse Heart cracks, the Heart of the light hangs in the air in front of Eli, close enough to take. He doesn't. He kneels and holds out open hands. In the town below, the nine pieces lift out of the lamp and rise up the Tower, and the light comes together on its own at the top. Sensei reads John 6:40 as written on the wall.
- **After:** the town is fully bright. Eli helps Sensei teach the newest white belts. Mara teaches beside him.
- **The last line, Sensei to the hero:** "The light found you on your first day. Now go carry it to someone else."

**Black belt degrees (summit tiers).** Short epilogues after launch: new students arrive, and the hero and Eli become the ones who go looking for the lost.

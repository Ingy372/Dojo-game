# Action Zone Curriculum: the Belt Ladder

Source: Jay, head instructor, 2026-10-18. This is the real curriculum the game mirrors. One belt in the dojo is one tier of the Tower.

**Related files:** the self-defense techniques are described in `docs/self-defense.md`. The verses, covenants, life words and creed are in `docs/faith-and-creed.md`.

## The ranks, in order

White → Yellow → Orange → Green → Blue → Red → Red with a black stripe → Brown → Brown with a black stripe → Black

That is 10 ranks and 9 promotions. Black belt degrees come after this (see docs/decisions.md).

## What each promotion requires

Every promotion requires 1 kata and the next 5 self-defense techniques. Promotions up to Red/Black → Brown also require 1 kick combination; the last two promotions before black belt have no new kick combo (Jay, 2026-10-19: the highest kick combo before black belt is #7). The self-defense count is cumulative: to earn orange, a student must know #1–10, not just the new five.

| Promotion | Kata | Self-defense (new) | Self-defense (must know) | Kick combo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| White → Yellow | Basic Form 1 | #1–5 | 5 | #1 |
| Yellow → Orange | Basic Form 2 | #6–10 | 10 | #2 |
| Orange → Green | Key On | #11–15 | 15 | #3 |
| Green → Blue | David and Goliath | #16–20 | 20 | #4 |
| Blue → Red | Pinon 1 | #21–25 | 25 | #5 |
| Red → Red/Black | Pinon 2 | #26–30 | 30 | #6 |
| Red/Black → Brown | Pinon 3 | #31–35 | 35 | #7 |
| Brown → Brown/Black | Nahanchi | #36–40 | 40 | none |
| Brown/Black → Black | Possi | #41–45 | 45 | none |

**At black belt,** students learn Kick Combos 8–12.

Each promotion also has a Bible verse, a covenant and a life word (see `docs/faith-and-creed.md`).

## What this means for the game

- **One Tower tier per rank,** with a Gate whose locks match this table exactly: 1 kata, 5 self-defense techniques, and a kick combo where the table lists one.
- **Kata unlocks a Form** (a stance), so 9 Forms are known (one per kata), plus the Beginner's Stance everyone starts with.
- **Each kick combo unlocks a Strike,** and each self-defense technique unlocks a Technique Seal (5 per tier).
- **Earlier techniques stay part of the requirement in real life** (the counts are cumulative). The game can reflect this: older Seals stay active and keep mattering in higher tiers, and Sensei's Question at the Bow can ask about any technique the student has earned, not only the newest.

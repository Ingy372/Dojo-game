# The Yellow Guardian: three ideas for Jay

Written Nov 25 by Claude Code. **Nothing here is in the game.** Pick one idea (or mix parts), change any words, and then it gets built and the Blender art made for your OK.

## Why this is needed

- White belt has its own floors and boss (the Floor Keeper). Orange has its own (the Hearth Cook in the Ember Kitchens).
- **Yellow has only its Tower art ("Sunrise Garden").** Yellow belts climb the shared rooms and meet the same Floor Keeper again at the top. That makes the Yellow months the weakest part of the Tower.
- When you approved the story (Nov 25), we agreed Claude would suggest three ideas.

## What every idea has to follow

- **The story's rule:** a Guardian is never a person. It is darkness that settled over a place where good work was done and took the shape of the worker. Beating it gets that place back, and that person's shop in town reopens.
- **The Yellow chapter, "Nobody Is Perfect":** the hero's own pride ("I've got this"), the dark floor nobody can cross alone, and Eli at the Gate, cold and hungry, when the hero shares food and a blanket. Covenant: Availability. Life word: Acceptance.
- **Fair fights:** every attack warns first (at least half a second, usually a full second), and only two things attack at once.
- **A step up from White, a step below Orange:** a little harder than the Floor Keeper (three moves instead of two) but easier than the Hearth Cook. Health somewhere between the two (about 520). The balance test sets the final numbers.
- Kid-facing lines are 8 words or fewer. **All lines below are drafts.**

## Idea 1: The Sun Gardener (the old bots' idea, cleaned up)

**The place:** the town garden. Its gardener keeps the flowers by the town square. When the light broke, darkness wrapped the garden statues in thorns.

**The fight (3 parts):**
1. **Thorn Whip:** it pulls a vine back (red circle), then whips. COUNTER it.
2. **Seed Rain:** glowing seeds hang in the air, then fall into a few red circles, one after another. Walk out of each.
3. **Vine Sweep:** a long striped arc around it ("!!" and a whoosh). DASH out.

**Story tie:** after the win, the garden in town blooms again and the gardener comes back to the square.

**Draft lines**
- Sensei, at the door: "The garden is tangled. Stay light."
- Gardener, in town after: "My flowers are opening again!"

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| The cheapest to build: its moves already exist in the game (circle, falling circles, striped arc). | Its moves feel a lot like the Hearth Cook's. It doesn't touch the Yellow story (pride, help, the blanket). |

## Idea 2: The Loom Keeper (Claude's pick)

**The place:** the tailor's shop, where the town's gis, blankets and belts were sewn. Darkness took the shape of the tailor: a tall figure of grey cloth with a giant spool.

**The fight (3 parts):**
1. **Spool Toss:** it lifts the spool high (red circle), then drops it. COUNTER it.
2. **Thread Lines:** it pulls dark threads tight across the room, one lane at a time (striped lanes, "!!", a twang). DASH across before they snap. Builds on the Hearth Cook's rolling-pin lane, but sideways and in a new order.
3. **The Big Cloth:** a dark cloth slowly slides over half the room. Its edge glows for a full second before it lands. Stay in the bright half. The danger is always drawn on top, so the cloth never hides another warning.

**Story tie (the strongest):** freeing it reopens the tailor's shop. The blanket the hero gives Eli at the Yellow Gate comes from there. So the boss fight leads straight into the chapter's big moment of sharing (Availability).

**Draft lines**
- Sensei, at the door: "Someone used to sew warm things here."
- Sensei, after: "Warm things are made to be shared."
- Tailor, in town after: "Take a blanket. Someone out there's cold."

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| A new fight that teaches staying out of moving danger, with a clear warning on every move. Fits the Yellow story best. | Middle cost: the Big Cloth is a new kind of attack (a slow half-room zone), plus a new Blender model. |

## Idea 3: The Lamplighter (the boldest)

**The place:** the street lamps. The lamplighter lit every lamp on the town's streets each evening. Darkness took his shape: a tall figure with a long unlit pole.

**The fight:** Sensei walks into the room holding a lantern, and it lights a circle around him. The Lamplighter's dark coat blocks every hit **outside** Sensei's light, so the hero has to fight near Sensei as he slowly crosses the room. Its moves: a Pole Swing (counter it), and Putting Out Lamps (dark circles that warn, then pop; walk out).

**Story tie:** this is the Yellow chapter's lesson as a fight: "I've got this" doesn't work here; walking with help does. It sits right after the scene where the hero waves Sensei off and falls. After the win, the town's street lamps come on.

**Draft lines**
- Sensei, at the door: "This time, we walk it together."
- Sensei, after: "Nobody climbs alone. Not even me."

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| The most meaningful: the fight itself teaches accepting help. | The most work: Sensei as a helper inside a fight and a moving circle of light are both new. It also comes close to the lamp of nine lights (the town lamp), which could confuse kids. |

## Claude's recommendation

**Idea 2, the Loom Keeper.** It is a new fight, it is fair, and it leads straight into the blanket scene. If you'd like the Idea 3 lesson too, Sensei's line at the Yellow door can carry it without building a helper system.

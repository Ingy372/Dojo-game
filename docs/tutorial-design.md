## 1. How the best games teach many systems

Games like Zelda: Breath of the Wild, Hades, Animal Crossing, Pokémon and Stardew Valley have dozens of systems, yet kids learn them without feeling buried. They use the same handful of patterns.

| Pattern | Who does it well | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **A small safe "starter area" that teaches the core moves, then opens the world.** | Zelda's Great Plateau | The tutorial floor teaches fighting only. When it ends, the world opens: your dojo, then the town. |
| **One new thing per day, given by a character, with a reason.** | Animal Crossing (the shop opens on day 2, the museum later), Stardew (a letter each morning) | A **First Week**: each day the student plays, Sensei unlocks one new thing. Kids come back to see what's new. |
| **Each system has a "home" you can see before it opens.** | Animal Crossing (the museum tent), Hades (rooms in the House you can't use yet) | Places you haven't learned yet are visible but **sleeping** (covered with a cloth, a little "Soon" tag). Tapping them: "Sensei will show you soon." No locked padlocks, no lists. |
| **Teach by doing, in four beats: show it, try it safe, try it for real, then add a twist.** | Nintendo's level design | Every lesson is: Sensei shows (1 line), you try in a safe spot, it shows up in a real run, then later a harder version appears. |
| **Just-in-time, not up front.** | Hades, Diablo | A system is taught the first time it matters (first chest, first stripe), never before. |
| **The screen grows with you.** | Pokémon, most mobile RPGs | Buttons appear only when they work. The first fight has only the stick and COUNTER. STRIKE appears when it's taught, DASH when it's taught. |
| **A "new" glow, not a list.** | Clash Royale, Genshin | A new button or place gets a soft glow and a gold dot until you tap it once. Nothing else nags. |
| **Every lesson can be seen again.** | Most premium games | The Book's Lessons shelf (already built) keeps every lesson with Replay. |
| **Always end on "what's next".** | Every good game | Each session ends with one clear next goal, never on a menu. |

Our own rules stay on top of these: no failing in the tutorial, one sentence per screen, never more than one new system per session (a session is a day with at least one run), and real achievements always get the biggest celebration.

## 2. The shape of the whole thing

| Part | When | Length | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The Tower Path (story)** | Day 1, first minute | 2 min | Why we're here, moving, dodging, the bow |
| **B. The tutorial floor (fighting)** | Day 1 | 6-8 min | Auto-attack, Counter, Strike, Dash, an Insight, a chest, a boss |
| **C. The Home Tour** | Day 1, right after | 4-5 min | Your dojo (a place to live), the town (a place to explore), the Training Board (how you go back to the Tower). **Places, not systems.** |
| **D. First real climb** | Day 1 | 5 min | Nothing new: just use what you learned. Ends on "come back tomorrow, I'll show you something." |
| **E. The First Week** | Days 2-8 that the student plays | 1 new thing a day | Gear, decorating, the Book, cleaning, the Path, the Shop, Fighting Style |
| **F. "First time it happens" lessons** | Whenever it first happens | 1 lesson each | Class Chest, Blessing run, first sign-off, first stripe and Virtue, first Form, Training Points, Missions, the Gate |
| **G. Belt-gated systems** | When the real belt reaches them | 1 lesson each | Street Smarts and the Monster Journal (2nd belt), quests and Honor (3rd), Challenge and elites (4th), later systems |
| **H. Existing students** | Their first week | 1 a day | Same tour on day 1; the systems they've earned introduced one a day (the Welcome Week, already built, follows the new order) |

The Home Tour is the answer to "don't break the one-system rule": it shows **where things are**, the way a friend shows you around a new house. It doesn't teach how anything works. Sleeping places wake up one per day in the First Week.

## 3. Part A: the Tower Path (day 1, about 2 minutes)

Already built, with Jay's Nov 18 fixes going in now:

1. Character creation, then story cards: the great light broke, Shadow slipped into the Tower.
2. Sensei: "The Tower is waking up. Come with me."
3. Walk to the glowing spot. **(Fix 1)** Sensei: "The Shadow feels you coming." then "It shakes the Tower! Stones are falling!"
4. **(Fix 2)** Five waves of stones: 1, 3, 3, 4, 5 stones, one always landing where you stand, short pauses. Growing chaos, never hurts (a stumble only), help after misses.
5. **(Fix 1)** Sensei: "It can't stop you. Bow at the door."
6. **(Fix 3)** A glowing mat appears in front of the Tower door. Stand on it; the hero faces the door and bows; the door opens and you walk in.

## 5. Part C: the Home Tour (day 1, 4-5 minutes)

Sensei walks with you (a glowing spot ahead, the same way the Tower Path does it). Each stop is one sentence and one tap or walk. Nothing to read twice.

### C1. Your dojo

| Stop | What happens | Sensei says (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Arrive | You walk in through your dojo's door. Soft celebration: your name on a little sign. | "Welcome home. This dojo is yours." |
| 2. The bow | Bow at the entrance mat (the dojo already asks for a bow). | "We always bow when we enter." |
| 3. Your first decoration | A gift box in the middle of the floor. Tap it: the Tower trophy from the boss you just beat. Sensei places it on the shelf for you (no decorating lesson yet). | "You earned this in the Tower." |
| 4. Sleeping spots | Three things under cloths with a little "Soon" tag: the bookshelf (the Book), the decorating chest, the broom corner. Walk past them; Sensei points once. | "I'll teach you these, one each day." |
| 5. The Training Station | The station glows. Don't open it yet. | "This is how you go back to the Tower." |
| 6. The door | A glowing path to the south door. | "Come, let's see the town." |

### C2. The town

| Stop | What happens | Sensei says (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Outside your dojo | You come out of your own house. The camera pulls back to show the street. | "This is our town." |
| 2. The great light | A big broken lantern in the square: the light from the story, dim. **New prop (art needed).** | "The light broke. Each floor you clear helps." |
| 3. The Action Zone | Walk to the Action Zone door. Its sign glows. | "This is our real dojo. Train here!" |
| 4. Sleeping places | Two or three buildings with "Soon" tags: Street Smarts house (opens at 2nd belt), a shop stall, classmates' houses (later). | "More places open as you grow." |
| 5. Back home | A glowing path back to your dojo door. | "Let's go home." |

The town tour shows the light from the story, so the town has a purpose from day 1: as the student climbs, the light in the square gets brighter (that matches "Street Smarts make the town brighter" later).

### C3. Back in your dojo, then the Training Board

| Stop | What happens | Sensei says (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The station | The Training Station glows. Walk to it. | "Ready? This is your Training Board." |
| 2. The Board | It opens with only **Enter the Tower** glowing. Gear and Style tabs show "Soon". | "Climb whenever you like." |
| 3. Release | Sensei's last line, then free play. | "I'm proud of you. Go climb!" |

The tutorial is officially over when the student taps Enter the Tower or leaves the station. A small scroll icon appears in the corner: "Lessons" (the Book's Lessons shelf, so any stop can be replayed).

## 6. Part D: the first real climb

No new systems. Ends with a short results card in the dojo, then Sensei: "Come back tomorrow. I'll show you something." This is the "end on anticipation" rule.

## 7. Part E: the First Week (one new thing per day played)

Each day the student plays, the first time they open the game Sensei wakes up one sleeping thing (the cloth comes off with a little sparkle, the "Soon" tag drops). One lesson, practice in a safe way, done. If they skip days nothing is lost; the next day they play gets the next item.

| Day played | What wakes up | Why this order | How it's taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Gear and Equipment** (Board's gear tab) | They'll have gear from day 1 in their bag: the most useful thing first | "Your bag has new gear. Try it on!" Tap Wear best. |
| 3 | **Decorating** (the decorating chest) | They have a trophy and maybe a decoration | Place one item. |
| 4 | **The Book** (bookshelf) | They've met monsters and moves to look back at | Open it, see your moves page. |
| 5 | **Cleaning** (the broom corner) | Dust has had time to show | Sweep one spot. Says plainly: dust is just for looks, never hurts. |
| 6 | **My Path and the Gate** | The real-training link: the most important, taught once they're comfortable | "This is your next real step in class." |
| 7 | **The Shop** (town stall) | They'll have coins by now | Buy one thing. |
| 8 | **Fighting Style** (Board's style tab) | Needs a sign-off or stripe to matter, so it's last | Only if they have something to set; otherwise it waits for part F. |

Combat lessons that already happen by themselves (Block, Step-out, Great Counter, VIRTUE ready) stay as they are: they fire the first time they happen and don't use up the day.

## 8. Part F: "the first time it happens" lessons

These wait for a real event. Each is one card plus one action, and counts as the day's new thing (if the day already had one, it waits until tomorrow, except real-achievement celebrations, which always play at once).

| Event | Lesson |
|---|---|
| First class attended (Class Chest and Blessing run) | "You went to class! Open your Class Chest." |
| First sign-off | Big real-achievement celebration first, then: "Your sign-off made you stronger in the Tower." |
| First stripe | Celebration, then the Virtue and the VIRTUE button. |
| First Form | Form switching. |
| First approved home practice | Training Points. |
| First Mission | Missions. |
| Reaching the Gate floor | "Only a real promotion opens this Gate." |

## 9. Part G: belt-gated systems

Kept as in core-design section 13: Street Smarts and the Monster Journal at 2nd belt (the town's Street Smarts house wakes up), quests and Honor at 3rd, Challenge and elites at 4th, later systems after that. Each is a sleeping place the student has seen since day 1.

## 10. Part H: existing students

- Day 1: the same Tower Path, tutorial floor and Home Tour, then the real-training cascade (everything they've earned in class unlocks with celebrations).
- Days 2+: the Welcome Week (already built) uses the First Week order above, plus the part F systems they already have (Virtues, Forms and so on), one a day.

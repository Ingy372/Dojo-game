# The Green Guardian: three ideas for Jay

Written Nov 26 by Claude Code. **Nothing here is in the game.** Pick one idea (or mix parts), change any words, and then it gets built and the Blender art made for your OK. Until then the Green floors (the Giant's Steps, switched off for players) end with the Floor Keeper as a marked stand-in.

## Why this is needed

- White has the Floor Keeper, Yellow the Loom Keeper, Orange the Hearth Cook. The story names the Green Guardian **the Stone Giant**, "a picture of a heart turned to stone".
- A Stone Giant file already exists (from the old bots), but it is only numbers: a stomp (circle), a stone toss (thrown circle) and a rock slide (lane). **Those are the same three kinds of move the Hearth Cook and the Loom Keeper already use.** Your main note on the Loom Keeper was that a new boss must not feel like the same fight, so each idea below is built around something no boss does yet.

## What the three bosses so far do (so Green can be different)

| Boss | How the fight works |
|---|---|
| Floor Keeper (White) | Walks up to you: a slam (circle) and a sweep (arc). Calls Swarmers at half health. |
| Loom Keeper (Yellow) | Keeps its distance and throws: spool circles, thread lanes, a half-room cloth. Climbs its loom while you break 3 pegs. |
| Hearth Cook (Orange) | Three phases: a ladle bonk (circle), a charge across the room, a rolling-pin lane. |

All three are about **moving out of danger and countering**. None changes the room, none is fought in pieces, and none asks you to stand still.

## What every idea has to follow

- **The story's rule:** a Guardian is never a person. It is darkness that settled over a place where good work was done and took the shape of the worker. Beating it gets that place back, and that person's shop in town reopens.
- **The Green chapter, "You Can't Earn It":** Eli's lumpy ball of glued sparks ("If I fix it, they'll have to take me back"), Sensei's "You can't earn the light. It was always a gift", Eli opening up (Openness), the peg still on the wall. Life word: Trust.
- **Fair fights:** every attack warns first (at least half a second, usually a full second) and can be escaped; only two things attack at once.
- **A step up from Orange:** a little harder than the Hearth Cook. The balance test sets the final health and damage (the Stone Giant file has 495 health at White-belt scale; the game scales it up for Green).
- Kid-facing lines are 8 words or fewer. **All lines below are drafts.**

## Idea 1: The Stone Giant's Hands (the story's Giant, made new)

**The place:** the town's stone steps and walls. The stonemason who built them laid every step by hand. Darkness took his shape and grew too big for the room.

**The fight:** the Giant is so tall that only its **two huge stone hands** reach down into the room (its face peeks over the top wall now and then). You never fight a body walking around.
1. **Hand Drop:** a hand's shadow grows on the floor for a full second, then the hand slams down and stays stuck for a moment. Walk out of the shadow, then hit the stuck hand.
2. **Pebble Brush:** a hand sweeps pebbles along the floor in a wide striped band ("!!"). DASH through or go around.
3. **Both Hands:** at half health both hands drop, one after the other (never at once). Hurt both hands enough and the Giant's face comes down to rest on the floor, dizzy, for the big finish.

**Story tie:** a heart of stone, too big to reach, softened one hand at a time. After the win the town's steps are mended and the stonemason is back at work.

**Draft lines**
- Sensei, at the door: "Big things fall one stone at a time."
- Sensei, after: "Even stone can soften."
- Stonemason, in town after: "I'll fix the steps. Mind the wet mortar!"

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| Feels huge without a giant sprite filling the phone screen. Two separate targets is new. Keeps the story's Stone Giant. | Middle cost: one boss made of two hands is new engine work (two hit targets that share health), plus a Blender model of the hands and face. |

## Idea 2: The Wall Builder (the room changes)

**The place:** the same stonemason's yard. Darkness took his shape: a short, broad figure with a stone trowel, always building.

**The fight:** it **builds low stone walls during the fight**, so the room keeps changing shape.
1. **Raise a Wall:** a glowing outline marks where a wall will rise, a full second ahead. Step off the outline. The wall cuts the room into lanes.
2. **Roll a Stone:** it rolls a stone down one lane (striped lane, "!!"). Change lanes or DASH.
3. **Hide and Mend:** it ducks behind a wall to mend it. A STRIKE breaks a wall, and behind it the Wall Builder is open for hits.

**Story tie:** walls are what Eli built around himself all winter. Breaking walls and finding the person behind them is Openness. After the win the town walls get a gate again.

**Draft lines**
- Sensei, at the door: "Walls keep people out. And in."
- Sensei, after: "A gate is a wall that opens."
- Stonemason, in town after: "I'm putting a gate in every wall."

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| A fight where the room itself changes, which no boss does. Uses STRIKE in a new way. Strong Openness picture. | The most engine work: walls that appear and break mid-fight, and pathing that copes with them. Lanes must never trap a child with no way out. |

## Idea 3: The Still Statue (stand still and trust)

**The place:** the statue garden by the town square. A sculptor carved every statue there. Darkness settled on her best statue and woke it.

**The fight:** the opposite of every other boss. The Statue **only sees you while you move**.
1. **The Watching Eye:** its eye glows and opens slowly (a full second, with a clear sound). While the eye is open, standing still is safe: it can't see you. Anyone who moves gets a stone flick (a circle, so there is still a way out).
2. **Turn Away:** when the eye closes it turns its back, and that is the time to run in and hit it.
3. **Shake Loose:** at half health small stones crack off it (the Swarmers' old job) and wander; they only move when the eye is closed, the same rule.

**Story tie:** this is "You Can't Earn It" as a fight. Trying harder and moving more is what gets you hit; being still and trusting the right moment is what works. After the win the statue garden is a garden again and the sculptor returns.

**Draft lines**
- Sensei, at the door: "Be still. Trust the moment."
- Sensei, after: "You didn't earn that. You trusted."
- Sculptor, in town after: "My statues look happier now. Don't you think?"

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| The most different fight of all: it teaches stillness, and the chapter's lesson lives in the rules. Cheapest of the three to build (one statue model, a new "eye open" rule). | A child who doesn't read the eye will think it's unfair at first, so the first eye needs Sensei's one-line lesson. It moves away from the story's name "Stone Giant" (the story text would change). |

## Claude's recommendation

**Idea 3, the Still Statue**, if a different fight matters most: it is unlike White, Yellow and Orange in every way, it is the cheapest, and it carries the Green lesson in its rules. **Idea 1** if you want to keep the story's Stone Giant as written. Idea 2 is the most work and the riskiest for fairness, so it is better saved for a later belt.

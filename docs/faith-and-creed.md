## 1. The Class Creed

Said together in class. Signed by Shinan Lowery.

> I intend to live by the code of the black belt.
> To seek God's truth. To put God first.
> To bring honor to the family.
> To show respect to my fellow man.
> To encourage and stand firm with him,
> doing all that I can to make the world a better place to live.
>

"More Than a Conqueror" is also the school's motto, printed on its training pads (from Romans 8:37).

## 2. How the system works

Every promotion carries three things:

- **A Bible verse.** Together, the verses walk through the Gospel in order: God's love (John 3:16), that everyone falls short (Romans 3:10 and 3:23), the gift of salvation (Romans 6:23 and 5:8), forgiveness (1 John 1:9), being saved, not condemned (John 3:17), being born again (John 3:3 and 3:5–8), and eternal life (John 6:40).
- **A covenant:** a promise the student makes to the people around them, written as "I will…"
- **A life word.** Read in belt order, the first letters of the life words spell **FAITH DOES**.

| Promotion | Verse | Covenant | Life word |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| White → Yellow | John 3:16 | Unconditional Love | **F** = Forgiveness |
| Yellow → Orange | Romans 3:10 | Availability | **A** = Acceptance |
| Orange → Green | Romans 3:23 | Prayer | **I** = Integrity |
| Green → Blue | Romans 6:23 | Openness | **T** = Trust |
| Blue → Red | Romans 5:8 | Honest | **H** = Honor |
| Red → Red/Black | 1 John 1:9 | Sensitivity | **D** = Discipline |
| Red/Black → Brown | John 3:17 | Confidentiality | **O** = Obedience |
| Brown → Brown/Black | John 3:3 | Accountability | **E** = Empathy |
| Brown/Black → Black | John 3:5–8 | *(none on the sign)* | **S** = Servanthood |
| Black Belt | John 6:40 | — | — |

## 3. The verses (exactly as on the signs)

**White → Yellow: John 3:16**
For God so loved the world that he gave his only begotten Son that whosoever believes in him shall not perish but have everlasting life.

**Yellow → Orange: Romans 3:10**
There is no one righteous, no not one.

**Orange → Green: Romans 3:23**
For all have sinned and falled short of the glory of God.

**Green → Blue: Romans 6:23**
For the wages of sin is death but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

**Blue → Red: Romans 5:8**
For God commendeth his love toward us in that while we were yet sinners Christ dies for us.

**Red → Red/Black: 1 John 1:9**
If we confess our sins he is faithful and just to forgive us of our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness.

**Red/Black → Brown: John 3:17**
For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world but to save the world through him.

**Brown → Brown/Black: John 3:3**
I tell you the truth, unless a man is born again he may not enter the kingdom of God.

**Brown/Black → Black: John 3:5–8**
I tell you the truth, unless a man is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. Flesh gives birth to flesh, but the Spirit gives birth to spirit. You should not be surprised at my saying, "You must be born again." The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.

**Black Belt: John 6:40**
For my Father's will is that everyone that looks to the Son and believes in him shall have eternal life, and I shall raise him up at that last day.

## 6. Questions for Jay before any of this becomes game text

1. **Wording on the signs.** Three lines look like they may differ from how they're taught. Should the game copy the signs exactly, or use corrected wording?
   - Romans 3:23: "falled short" (usually "fall short" or "come short")
   - Romans 5:8: "Christ dies for us" (usually "Christ died for us")
   - Confidentiality: a word on the sign is covered over. Should the game leave it out, as the sign does?
2. **Brown/Black → Black covenant.** The sign shows a verse and a life word but no covenant. Is there one?
3. **Bible translation.** The signs mix the King James wording ("commendeth," "begotten") with modern wording ("I tell you the truth"). Should the game keep each verse exactly as on its sign?
4. **The life words and the stripe words.** The stripe words (Respect, Great Effort, Self-Discipline) already power the game's Virtues. Are the FAITH DOES life words a separate thing students learn for each promotion, and is either one tested?
5. **What students must know.** For each promotion, do students recite the verse, the covenant and the life word, or are they taught and discussed? This decides whether they become Gate locks, like a kata, or are learned through play.

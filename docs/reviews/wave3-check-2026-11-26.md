# Wave-3 check (Nov 26, 2026)

**Question (Grok playtest of Nov 25, section 4 item 4):** every run the bot played died in wave 3 (Yellow rooms have 3 waves, Orange rooms have 4). Is wave 3 a difficulty spike?

**Answer: no. Nothing in the rooms was changed.** For a student with the strength their real belt gives them, every wave is gentle and wave 3 is no harder than waves 1–2. The boss is the hardest fight, as it should be. The bot died because its hero had a White belt's strength (Level 1 after a grown-up belt switch) and it was mashing buttons. That is finding 1 of the report, not a room problem.

## How it was measured

The balance bots (`packages/sim/test/balance/wave3-check.test.ts`) played 40 full floors for each setup. The hero had the average stats of a trained student at that belt (the same "typical" numbers the other balance tests use). Three kinds of player:

- **typical**: a normal 10–12 year old who times Counters fairly well
- **novice**: a younger or newer player, slower and less exact
- **kid**: a novice who also presses random buttons (a masher, closest to how the Grok bot played)

The bots measured how much Health was lost in each wave of each room, as a share of full Health.

Run it again with: `BALANCE=1 RUNS=40 npx vitest run test/balance/wave3-check --silent=false` (in `packages/sim`). Add `STATS_BELT=1` to give the hero a White belt's stats.

## Results with the real belt's strength

Average Health lost per wave, across all rooms of the floor:

| Floor | Mode | Player | Wave 1 | Wave 2 | Wave 3 | Wave 4 | Boss | Floor cleared |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow | Guided | typical | 0% | 0% | 0% | – | 3% | 100% |
| Yellow | Guided | kid (masher) | 3% | 3% | 10% | – | 36% | 100% |
| Yellow | Standard | typical | 1% | 1% | 2% | – | 6% | 100% |
| Yellow | Standard | novice | 2% | 2% | 4% | – | 7% | 100% |
| Orange | Guided | typical | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% | 100% |
| Orange | Guided | kid (masher) | 2% | 5% | 7% | 9% | 14% | 95% |
| Orange | Standard | typical | 0% | 1% | 1% | 1% | 4% | 100% |
| Orange | Standard | novice | 1% | 3% | 1% | 2% | 10% | 100% |

- No room had a wave where a typical or novice player lost more than 5% of their Health on average.
- **The last wave of a room is a little heavier for mashers** (on Yellow, wave 3 costs them about 10% against 3% for each earlier wave). That's expected: the next wave comes in while one enemy is still left, so the last wave also has the leftovers, and it is the longest wave. It is still far easier than the boss and caused no deaths on Guided.
- Mashing on **Standard** fails at every belt (0% of floors cleared). That's intended: Standard asks for real Counters, and the game already suggests Guided after two losses.

## Results with a White belt's strength (what the Grok bot had)

| Floor | Mode | Player | Wave 1 | Wave 2 | Wave 3 | Wave 4 | Floor cleared |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow | Guided | kid (masher) | 11% | 11% | 32% | – | 3% |
| Orange | Guided | kid (masher) | 10% | 37% | 45% | 35% | 0% |
| Orange | Standard | typical | 3% | 11% | 6% | 8% | 78% |

This matches what the Grok bot saw: a weak hero who mashes buttons dies late in the first rooms, mostly in wave 3. With the belt's real strength, the same masher clears Yellow Guided 100% of the time and Orange Guided 95% of the time.

## What this means

- The rooms don't need softening. A real Yellow or Orange student who arrives through promotions has the strength for them.
- The real issue is finding 1 of the report: the grown-up "Belt & stripes" switch leaves the hero at Level 1. A student who really was promoted wouldn't arrive that way. That fix belongs to the session working on finding 1, not to the room data.
- Worth watching in real playtests: kids who mash on Guided lose most Health in a room's last wave. If real kids feel this too, the gentlest fix would be one enemy fewer in each room's last wave (a change to the room files only). Not changed now, because the numbers don't call for it.

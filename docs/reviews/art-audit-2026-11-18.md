# Art audit, Nov 18 2026 (Claude Code)

Jay's ask: score every piece of art in the game; anything under the bar goes on a rework list. The goal is "the ABC Mouse of martial arts" for ages 6-14: bright, friendly, premium.

**How it was scored.** Every art pack in `assets/art/` was laid out on contact sheets (first frame of each sprite, facing the camera) and looked at in the game at phone size (844x390). Score out of 10 against a premium kids' app (think Archero 2, Toca Boca, ABC Mouse):

- **8-10** premium: keep.
- **7** good: keep, small polish later.
- **5-6** fine but plain: rework when its area comes up.
- **1-4** not good enough: rework.

**The bar is 7.** Everything under 7 is on the rework list.

## Rework list (under 7), in the order I recommend

Highest impact first: what every child sees in the first 10 minutes, then what they see every day.

1. **Tier 1 enemies (score 4)**: brighter, friendlier monsters with readable faces and a light rim, bigger frames. First thing every child fights. Same Blender rig approach as the hero so the animations carry over.
2. **Home Dojo room and mats (3-4)**: a warm wooden floor with real grain and light, a wall with windows and soft daylight, proper mats with stitched edges. Must stay "bare to start" (Jay: students decorate it), so it's the *material* that improves, not more stuff.
3. **The town (6, plus Jay's ask)**: a wider town rendered from the Blender town file (`incoming-art/town-square-concept/source/town-square_town.blend`) at twice the detail, about twice as wide, so it scrolls sideways and stays sharp when zoomed in.
4. **Hearth Cook (4)**: a proper character model with a big phase-2 change (steam, bigger, glowing apron), per Jay's two-phase rule.
5. **Tower floors and walls, tiers 1-3 (5-6)**: textured stone, garden and kitchen floors with light and wear, so rooms feel like places.
6. **Action Zone inside and outside (3-4)**: rebuild in Blender with the same kit; the real dojo should look as good as the home dojo.
7. **Insight and Seal icons (5)**: one shared premium badge style (glossy, coloured by family), so they read as collectible.
8. **Dojo props and tier-1 decorations (6)**: re-render with light and shadow; keep the designs.
9. **Orange enemies and the Bopper (5)**: more detail and character.
10. **Book and Path screens**: restyle to the violet glass look so every menu matches.

Each one is a Blender job (or code for the menus) that Jay approves before it goes in, one at a time, like the run and the chest.

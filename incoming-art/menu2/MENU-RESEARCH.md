# What makes top mobile menus feel premium (notes for A-MENU2)

These are notes only. No assets were copied. We take the principles and render them in our own dojo materials (wood, washi, brass).

**Clash Royale**
- Chunky, saturated, pre-rendered-looking depth: thick bevels, a top highlight and a dark bottom lip on every button.
- One colour role per job: yellow is the primary action and green is the secondary. You never have to guess.
- Small animated highlights (a glint sweep or a pulsing next-step) pull the eye without any text.
- Popups are cards over the screen, not fullscreen takeovers, so you never lose your place.

**Genshin Impact**
- Frames get richer as rarity goes up: more trim, more gold and more glow. You can read rarity before you read any words.
- Fine gold corner flourishes on a dark, quiet ground make the content glow.
- Generous padding and consistent corner radii.

**Pokémon Scarlet/Violet**
- Selection is unmistakable: a strong outline, a lift and a contrast change. Kids never wonder what's picked.
- Icons always come with a label, and each icon has a strong silhouette.
- The reward moment uses a few sparks and a short sweep. As they say, "not too few, not too many".

**Hades**
- Icon-first, with strict colour discipline: each colour means one thing.
- Rarity frames, plus a themed material for every surface.
- Animation is short and themed (sweeps and flickers), not generic bounces.

## How we apply this
- **Material language:** the outer frame is warm wood, contents sit on washi paper, and brass is used only for accents and rewards (the seal, studs, brackets). That's our version of "gold means value".
- **Rarity:** frame complexity scales with rarity. Common is plain wood. Uncommon gets 2 brass studs. Rare gets 4 studs and 2 stars. Epic gets 8 studs and 4 stars. Colours come from the game's rarity table.
- **States:** every interactive piece has normal, selected (a gold rim plus a warm glow), pressed (down 1 px, darker) and disabled or not-yet (a faint ghost on paper with a dashed outline, never grey shame).
- **Guidance:** one lantern glows on the next cubby only. A single cue, never a nag.
- **Reward moments:** unlock-sweep (8 frames) and optional shine (6 frames). They're cheap, short and kid-calm.

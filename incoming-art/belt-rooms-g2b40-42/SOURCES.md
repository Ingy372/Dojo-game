# Sources

These rooms were built in Blender for Dojo Ascent. No art pack was bought, and nothing here is imported into the game yet.

- Camera and toon shader: `incoming-art/tools/toon_kit.py` and `incoming-art/tools/render_static.py` (orthographic, 35° down, 80 px per tile).
- The three approved sample rooms stay in `sprites/`. The full kit reuses that same look.
- Room shapes, names, and prop lines are copied from `drafts/content/rooms/` (`giants-steps`, `quiet-river`, `forge-of-courage`, `hall-of-echoes`, `root-library`, `storm-stair`, `summit`). No new room was invented.
- The fighter on the contact sheets is the approved kitchen player (`incoming-art/player-character/sprites`), white gi, orange belt.
- Colours follow each brief. Where a brief says deep red or a red lid, the paint is lacquer brown, because danger-red is only for attack warnings.

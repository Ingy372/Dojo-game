# R-PREMIUM-b: free sound effect packs for Dojo Ascent

## Delivery Note
- **ITEM:** R-PREMIUM-b, sound effect research (no audio downloaded or committed).
- **WHAT:** a shortlist of 7 free packs. Each one's license was checked on its live page on 2026-10-26. The packs cover hits, punches and kicks, blocks, UI taps and clicks, and reward and level-up chimes.
- **WHY:** Dojo Ascent is a warm, kid-friendly (6+) martial-arts mobile RPG that will be sold. Every pick allows commercial use in a sold app, with no NC clause, no share-alike, and no attribution required (CC0, or a royalty-free EULA).
- **DECIDE (Jay's taste pick):**
  - Soft and warm (Kenney Impact plus Interface plus Jingles), or punchier and more "real" (Sonniss GDC).
  - Whether a light 8-bit flavour (512 SFX) fits the art for rewards.
  - How cartoony the hits should feel for ages 6 and up.
- **NEXT:** Jay picks a direction. Then we download only the chosen files, audition them at phone volume, and keep a `LICENSES.md` noting the source and license of every file we ship.

**Top 3:** 1) Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0), 2) Kenney Interface Sounds (CC0), 3) Kenney Music Jingles (CC0).

File names below come from the packs' documented contents. Check them against the zip when downloading.

---

## 1. Kenney: Impact Sounds (top pick for hits and blocks)
- **Link:** https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (stated on the page). https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Covers:**
  - Hits, punches and kicks: yes.
  - Blocks: yes, using the soft, wood and plate impacts.
- **Sample files:** `impactPunch_medium_000-004.ogg` (punch or kick hits), `impactPunch_heavy_*.ogg` (finishers), `impactSoft_medium_*.ogg` (kid-friendly light hits), `impactWood_medium_*.ogg` (a block on a pad or makiwara), `impactPlank_medium_*.ogg` (board break), `footstep_wood_*.ogg` (dojo floor).
- **Caveats:**
  - These are realistic one-shots. Some may need pitching up or a short tail for a warmer feel.
  - Skip the metal and glass impacts: they read as harsh.

## 2. Kenney: Interface Sounds (top pick for UI)
- **Link:** https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Covers:**
  - UI taps and clicks: yes.
  - Small reward pings: yes.
- **Sample files:** `click_001-005.ogg`, `select_001-008.ogg`, `confirmation_001-004.ogg` (reward or confirm), `toggle_*.ogg`, `pluck_*.ogg` (soft and friendly), `maximize_*.ogg` / `minimize_*.ogg` (panel open and close), `error_*.ogg` (gentle "can't do that").
- **Caveats:** none on the license. Pick one family (`pluck` or `click`) so the UI stays consistent.

## 3. Kenney: Music Jingles (top pick for rewards and level-up)
- **Link:** https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Covers:**
  - Reward chimes: yes.
  - Level-up chimes: yes.
  - Victory or defeat stingers: yes.
- **Sample files:** `jingles_PIZZI00-16.ogg` (light and playful, the best fit for kids), `jingles_STEEL*.ogg` (bright chime), `jingles_NES*.ogg` (retro option), `jingles_SAX*.ogg`, `jingles_HIT*.ogg`.
- **Caveats:**
  - Skip the minor-key "lose" jingles for kid-facing failure. Use a soft one instead.

## 4. Kenney: RPG Audio
- **Link:** https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- **License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Covers:** this is for the incidentals around the main needs: gi swishes and pickups.
- **Sample files:** `cloth1-4.ogg` (gi swish or whoosh before a kick), `handleCoins.ogg` / `handleCoins2.ogg` (coin rewards), `bookFlip*.ogg` (menus or scrolls), `metalClick.ogg`, `doorOpen_*.ogg` (dojo doors).
- **Caveats:**
  - It has blade and knife sounds (`knifeSlice*`, `drawKnife*`). Don't use them: we use wooden and padded gear only.

## 5. OpenGameArt: "100 CC0 SFX" by rubberduck
- **Link:** https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
- **License:** CC0 (marked on the page). https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Covers:**
  - Hits: partly.
  - Reward chimes: yes, from the bells and gongs.
  - Splashes: also included, useful for the town river.
- **Sample files (from the page's list):** 3x bell (reward chime), 2x "gong" (dojo gong for a round start or level-up), 4x hit, 3x wooden effect (block or makiwara), 2x "plop" (soft UI), 2x splash (river).
- **Caveats:**
  - It was recorded on a phone, so quality varies. Audition every file.
  - One commenter reported a corrupted zip.
  - Skip the "toilet flushing", "shot", "explosion" and "weird" files.

## 6. OpenGameArt: "512 Sound Effects (8-bit style)" by SubspaceAudio
- **Link:** https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style
- **License:** CC0 (marked on the page, and the author confirms in the comments). https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Covers:**
  - UI taps: yes.
  - Reward and level-up: yes.
  - Hits: in 8-bit style only.
- **Sample files (pack categories):** `General Sounds/Coins/sfx_coin_single*.wav` (rewards), `General Sounds/Fanfares/` (level-up), `General Sounds/Menu Sounds/sfx_menu_select*.wav` / `sfx_menu_move*.wav` (UI), `General Sounds/Simple Damage Sounds/` (hits).
- **Caveats:**
  - This is a retro chiptune style, which may clash with the warm painted look. Use it only if Jay likes a retro accent.
  - The bigger 1000- and 6000-sound packs on itch.io are paid and have separate terms.

## 7. Sonniss: #GameAudioGDC Bundle (the free yearly bundles)
- **Link:** https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc
- **License:** "#GameAudioGDC Bundle Licensing Agreement – Unlimited User License", v2.0, effective 27 Aug 2026. https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/
  - It allows royalty-free commercial use in games and apps with no attribution.
  - Each download is governed by the version of the license published on the day you download.
- **Covers:**
  - Hits, punches and kicks: yes, pro quality.
  - Whooshes and blocks: yes.
  - Some UI and chime libraries, depending on the year.
- **Sample content to look for (it varies by year):** foley "body punch", "cloth whoosh", "wood impact" and "martial arts" libraries, and the "UI" and "magic chime" libraries. Search the bundle's folder names after downloading.
- **Caveats:**
  - It's very large (tens of GB per year), and the sounds are often realistic or violent. Curate hard for ages 6 and up.
  - You can't redistribute the files as sound effects, so they must not sit in a public repo or ship as a loose asset pack. Keep them inside the built app or a private repo only.
  - AI training on the files is prohibited.

## Checked but not shortlisted
- **freesound.org:** the license is set per file and is often CC-BY or CC-BY-NC. Use only items filtered to "Creative Commons 0" and record each file's URL. No single pack beat the Kenney packs for our needs.
- **"Fantasy Sound Effects Library"** (Little Robot Sound Factory, OpenGameArt): CC-BY, so it needs attribution. Left out to avoid attribution obligations.

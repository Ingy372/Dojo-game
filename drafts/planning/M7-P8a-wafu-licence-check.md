## Do the volumes differ?
**No, apart from the title and some example text.** Three lines change between volumes:
- the title line;
- the example line under "Modify, edit, remix…" (temple cues, zone crossfades, or boss/spell card switching);
- the cue type in the AI note ("temple" / "exploration" / "shooter").

Every rule is word-for-word the same in all three. Each one says: "The license below is the same one that ships with every paid WAFU volume."

## Exact quotes (Vol.35; Vol.29 matches)
**The grant:** "you are granted the following non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license"

**Commercial use:** "Use the music and cues in unlimited commercial and non-commercial projects: video games, apps, videos, streams, films, podcasts, tabletop projects, and similar media." Also: "Keep using the music forever. No recurring fees, no royalties."

**Redistribution and sublicensing ban:** "Resell, redistribute, or sublicense the audio files themselves, whether original or modified, as standalone audio (e.g. in another asset pack, sample library, sound effect library, or template)."

**Other bans:**
- "Upload the tracks to music streaming or distribution platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Content ID, etc.) as your own work."
- "Claim authorship of the original compositions."
- "Use the audio files to train, fine-tune, or seed generative AI models."

**Credit:** "Not required. If you would like to credit, "Music: WAFU Sound Works" is appreciated."

**AI disclosure:** "These tracks were produced with the assistance of generative AI (Suno, commercial plan), then curated, edited, loop-engineered, and level-matched by a human."

**Warranty:** "This pack is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind."

**Apps sold or licensed to other dojos, white-label, rebranded builds:** **the licence never mentions these words.** It names no third parties or clients and says nothing about rebranding or transferring the licence.

## Verdict (plain words, not legal advice)
- **Selling the finished game, music inside, in the app stores: allowed.** "apps" and "commercial" are named, and the ban only covers the files "as standalone audio".
- **Selling or licensing our builds to other dojos, including rebranded ones that we make: looks allowed, but it's not written down.** The ban is on standalone audio, and a game build isn't standalone audio. But the licence is "granted" to the person who downloads it and doesn't talk about clients.
- **Giving dojos the music files, or the project/template, to rebuild themselves: risky, likely forbidden.** "template" and "sublicense … as standalone audio" are named in the ban.
- **Sublicensing:** banned only for the audio files on their own. Nothing is said about sublicensing the music as part of a game.

**Overall: UNCLEAR, leaning allowed** for white-label builds that Jay makes and ships. For certainty, get a short written yes from WAFU, and keep it with the lawyer check before milestone 8.

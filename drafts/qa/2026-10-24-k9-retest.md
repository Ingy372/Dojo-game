## New bugs
1. **COMBO count sits on top of the boss bar**
   - What I did: in the boss room (`?tower=t2&room=keepers-hall&seed=3`), I let 2 hits land.
   - What happened: the gold "2 COMBO" text draws right over the purple boss bar and the "Floor Keeper" label, at both sizes. This is new, because the bar moved up into the combo's spot. `/workspace/qa/k9/667x375-keeper-3000.png`, `/workspace/qa/k9/844x390-keeper-guide-3000.png`
   - What should have happened: both are readable. Move the combo count lower when a boss bar shows.
   - Severity: **SMALL**
2. **Sensei banner covers the HOME door label in the boss room**
   - What I did: same room, during Sensei's Counter guide.
   - What happened: the banner sits over the "HOME" door label just under the boss name. Same screenshots as bug 1.
   - What should have happened: the label stays visible, or the banner sits a bit lower.
   - Severity: **SMALL**
3. **The ?elite=guarding test link still puts Guarding on Guided** (test link only)
   - What I did: started a run with `forcedElite: guarding` on Guided, in the sim.
   - What happened: it spawned a Guarding elite. Players can't reach this, but a tester could be misled.
   - What should have happened: either it's fine as a test tool (just note it), or it should respect Guided.
   - Severity: **SMALL**
4. **The store script's notes field went back to an old "DRAFT … nothing here ships until Jay approves" text** (PR #82)
   - What I did: read the content diff.
   - What happened: the trimmed file replaced the "approved through J11" note with the draft note. Players never see it, but it confuses the record.
   - What should have happened: the note says it's approved (J11) and trimmed (#79).
   - Severity: **SMALL**

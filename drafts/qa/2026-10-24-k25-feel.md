## Bugs (most serious first)
B1 ANNOYING — Wind-up sound ends early. Did: read GameScene 1122 `sfx.windup(ev.ticks / TICKS_PER_SECOND)`. Happened: the rising tone still lasts the old (fast) wind-up time, so it finishes ~12% before the swing (≈130 ms early on a 1 s wind-up). Kids who counter on the sound's peak will be early. Should: use realMsPerTick() (tone = real wind-up length).
B2 SMALL — Warning timer uses the old tick length (line 1129 `until`), so "two attacks at once" (session-2 Dash lesson trigger) slightly under-fires. Should: realMsPerTick().
B3 SMALL — Challenge timer text counts sim seconds; a "60 s" timer now takes ~68 real seconds. It's kinder, not harmful; decide if the label is OK.
B4 SMALL/risk — Brown rule matches the belt id 'brown'. A school whose belt list has no 'brown' id never gets the hit shake except on Challenge. Fine for current fixtures.

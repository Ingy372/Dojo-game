## Issues (most serious first)
**1. Blessing glow fades almost out (SMALL)**
- **What I did:** I blessed the profile and sampled the glow every 250 ms for 6.5 s.
- **What happened:** The object's alpha goes from 1.0 to 0.15. The fill alpha is already 0.25, so the visible glow goes from 0.25 down to about 0.04. On a dark background the glow is hard to see at its low point. In the screenshot it barely shows.
- **Should have happened:** The tokens say breath `from 0.25 to 0.15`, which reads like a gentle dip that is always visible. The tween animates object alpha, but the 0.25 was set as the fill alpha. Either set the fill to 1 and keep the 0.25→0.15 tween, or confirm that the deep fade is what you want.

**2. Continue button on the run-summary card is still the old style (SMALL)**
- **What I did:** I read the Home code and dismissed the card after a run.
- **What happened:** "Continue" (HomeScene around line 208) is still a square rectangle. It has no press feedback and no tap sound. It also acts on finger-up even when the finger slid onto it.
- **Should have happened:** It should behave like the other Home buttons. Its size is 160x42, so the tap height is just under 44.

**3. Gear item cards and the hidden "Training Board" title use plain taps (SMALL)**
- **What happened:** The item cards are square, with no press feedback and no tap sound. A finger that slides onto a card and lifts still selects it. The title is a hidden testing tap, which is fine to leave alone.
- **Should have happened:** Probably P-MENU-b scope. I'm noting it so "every Home button" is accurate.

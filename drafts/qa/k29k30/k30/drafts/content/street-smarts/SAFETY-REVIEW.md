# C1 Street Smarts: items for Jay's safety check

Scenario: `store-separated.json` (ages 6-9, store, "separated from your grown-up").
Nothing here ships until Jay approves it (framework section 8).

## What the scenario teaches
1. Stay where you are. Don't run around, and don't go outside.
2. Find a safe helper: a store worker with a name tag, or the cashier.
3. Use your voice: "I can't find {grownup}. Can you help me?"
4. Say your grown-up's name.
5. Never leave the store with someone who says they'll take you to your grown-up.

## Please check each one
1. **Who counts as a safe helper?** The draft offers only a worker with a name tag and the cashier. Should "a mom or dad with kids" also be a right answer? It is common advice, but it is harder to show in a picture, so I left it out.
2. **The shopper line** "I saw your grown-up outside. Come with me." The shopper is drawn as an ordinary person and never threatens anyone. Is this OK for ages 6+, or should it be cut from the 6-9 version?
3. **Sensei's rule line** "We never leave the store with someone else." This is the only line that states a rule outright. Is it OK, given that Sensei shouldn't lecture?
4. **Code word.** The draft does not mention a family code word. Add it, or leave it for class and parents?
5. **Grown-up's name.** `{grownupName}` would need a new field that the player or a parent fills in. Should it be a real name (for example "Maria Lopez") or just "Mom/Dad"? There's no real student data in the file. Should we also teach a phone number? I left phone numbers out on purpose.
6. **`{grownup}` word.** Who chooses Mom, Dad, Grandma, and so on: the player, or a parent setting? Or should every player see one neutral word, "your grown-up"?
7. **Reunion on screen.** The grown-up comes back happy and hugs the player, with no scolding. Show it, or end with the worker's announcement?
8. **"Go outside to look"** is shown as a wrong choice. Its reflection only asks "Where is {grownup} most likely to be?" and never mentions the parking lot. Is that enough?
9. **Store speaker announcement** ("The worker calls on the store speaker"). Is that realistic enough for kids here?
10. **Two-sentence screens.** These break the one-sentence rule: "I can help. What is your grown-up's name?", "Thank you. Let's stay here together.", "That's right. We'll wait right here.", "There you are! I'm so glad you stayed.", and Sensei's summary line. Split them, or keep them?
11. **Stars.** One star each for stay, helper, and refusing the offer. Wrong choices cost nothing and you retry right away. OK?
12. **Match to class.** Does this match what you teach in class? Could it be a Scenario of the Month?

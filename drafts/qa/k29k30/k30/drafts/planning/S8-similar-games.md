# S8: Similar games study

Work board S8: for each listed game, what Dojo Ascent should borrow and what to avoid.

Author: Dojo-Ascent Researcher & Planner, 2026-10-24.

**How to read this:**

- Every fact about a game links to its source. The sources are reviews, developer interviews, official help pages and design write-ups, all read on 2026-10-24.
- Lines marked "**For us:**" are my design suggestions, not facts.
- No learning-science claims; the Learning Designer owns that.
- A game's numbers are quoted only when the source gives them. Monetization numbers can change after the source date.

## Each game

### Hades (Supergiant Games)

**Facts:**

- God Mode gives 20% damage resistance, +2% after each death, up to 80%. It can be switched on or off any time and doesn't weaken enemies ([Inverse interview](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview); [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/2020/10/6/21504219/hades-god-mode-difficulty/)).
- The team's mantra was "every run counts". They wanted many channels of permanent progression and no feeling of "game over" ([Tom's Guide interview](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** S5's Guided offer works in the same spirit: help that's offered kindly, with no shame. "Every run counts" matches S7 (the meter only fills) and S9.
- **Avoid:** Kasavin says God Mode is designed for Hades' structure and may not transfer ([Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview)). So we keep our own lever, the counter window, rather than copying damage resistance.

### Archero (Habby)

**Facts:**

- Each run costs 5 of 20 energy, and 1 energy recharges every 12 minutes. Energy can be bought with gems or refilled by watching ads ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/finding-the-fun-archero-part-3---monetization)).
- Chests opened with gems work as loot boxes ([Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/archero-advertising)).
- Each run offers random abilities to pick from, and they reset each run ([Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/archero-advertising)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** short runs and picking from a small set of upgrades (our Insights).
- **Avoid:** all of the energy, ads and gem chests. These are already banned by our rules.

### Soul Knight (ChillyRoom)

**Facts:**

- A free roguelike shooter with random dungeons and many weapons.
- Gems can be earned or bought, and ads can be watched for gems or revives ([TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/2017/03/10/soul-knight-review); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/soul-knight/id1184159988?l=en-GB)).
- Reviewers noted repetitive runs and unclear stats ([The Outerhaven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/2017/03/soul-knight-android-review/)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** easy-to-learn controls on a phone, and variety from combining systems (room twists).
- **Avoid:** ads for revives. Also avoid unclear stats: show plain numbers on the Power Rating.

### Ring Fit Adventure (Nintendo)

**Facts:**

- An RPG where battles are won by doing real exercises. Levels take 5–10 minutes, and exercise cooldowns push variety ([Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/reviews/2019/10/18/20920926/ring-fit-adventure-review-nintendo-switch-fitness-game/)).
- Reviewers called the forced grinding and long boss fights tedious or frustrating ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/17/20917908/nintendo-switch-ring-fit-adventure-review); [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/reviews/2019/10/18/20920926/ring-fit-adventure-review-nintendo-switch-fitness-game/)).
- The Verge reviewer said he felt guilty about missed days ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/17/20917908/nintendo-switch-ring-fit-adventure-review)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** this is the closest model: real effort becomes in-game power. Our version is real class, sign-offs and Training Points.
- **Borrow:** short sessions.
- **Avoid:** level gates that force grinding. Keep bosses short. Never use guilt wording (S9 already bans it).

### Pokémon GO (Niantic)

**Facts:**

- Adventure Sync counts walking through Apple Health or Google Fit even when the app is closed. It can hatch eggs and earn Buddy candy ([Niantic Help Center](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/3265-adventure-sync/); [official post](https://pokemongolive.com/en/post/adventure-sync)).
- A weekly summary gives rewards at distance tiers ([Switchblade Gaming guide](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/pokemon-go/adventure-sync-guide/)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** real activity counts even if the game isn't open. We already do this: late feed classes still count (S7). Weekly summaries are a calm rhythm.
- **Avoid:** time-limited event pressure and reminder pushes. S7's Open House rule already returns missed event pieces.

### Animal Crossing: New Horizons (Nintendo)

**Facts:**

- It uses the real clock for seasons, shop hours and building, which makes for a slow routine ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2020/03/animal-crossing-new-horizons-review-a-quarantined-life-has-never-been-cuter/)).
- Nook Miles give small optional goals for everyday actions ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2020/3/25/21192570/animal-crossing-new-horizons-nook-miles-addition)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** the calm Home Dojo, decorating, and small optional goals (the S9 goal line, S6 Goals page).
- **Avoid:** making things wait on real-time clocks or daily checks. That would act as a daily-login pull, which is against our rules.

### Pokémon (main series, e.g. Scarlet and Violet)

**Facts:**

- Completing the Paldea Pokédex (400 entries) earns the Shiny Charm ([official Trainers Guide](https://scarletviolet.pokemon.com/en-us/trainers-guide/complete-your-paldea-region-pokedex/); [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/pokemon-scarlet-and-violet/what-do-you-get-for-completing-the-pokedex-in-pokemon-scarlet-violet/)).
- The Shiny Charm raises shiny odds from 1/4096 to about 1/1365 ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-scarlet-violet-shiny-hunting-odds-charm-methods-1984591/)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** a collection log with a sure reward. That's our Monster Journal (S6 `ach.journal.*`, S7 source C).
- **Avoid:** "rare by chance" as the main prize. Our sure-path rule (S7-rev) covers this.

### Duolingo

**Facts:**

- Duolingo says its streak uses loss aversion. It also offers Streak Freezes, because breaking a streak can be demotivating ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/)).
- A product teardown argues the freeze and repair features are sold on top of the anxiety the streak creates ([NextLeap teardown, PDF](https://assets.nextleap.app/submissions/Duolingo_Teardown-1d547392-f4ca-4510-867c-ba18f0388c7a.pdf)).
- An ACM paper on children and gamified learning apps reports that children read breaks being framed as damage as "punishment" ([ACM paper, PDF](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3773077.3806127)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** visible progress and flexibility.
- **Avoid:** any reset to 0, "at risk" messages, or protection for sale. Our count-up `weeksTrained` (S9) is the alternative.

### Prodigy Math

**Facts:**

- In February 2021, 22 advocacy groups led by the Campaign for a Commercial-Free Childhood asked the FTC to investigate Prodigy ([Fairplay press release](https://fairplayforkids.org/feb-19-2021-advocates-to-ftc-prodigy-math-game-preys-on-kids-and-families/); [complaint PDF](https://fairplayforkids.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Prodigy_Complaint_Feb21.pdf)). The complaint alleges:
  - a "free" label while marketing a paid membership to kids
  - membership-only items shown during play
  - visible differences between members and non-members
- A letter from Senator Markey's office describes members' avatars getting a cloud and an "M" ([Markey letter, PDF](https://www.markey.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/(2.26.2021)%20Letter%20--%20FTC%20Prodigy%20--%202-25-21.pdf)).
- Prodigy said most users play free ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/02/20/this-week-in-apps-sneak-peek-at-tiktok-shopping-new-ios-and-android-betas-kids-app-prodigy-hit-with-ftc-complaint/)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** battles that come from real learning.
- **Avoid:** any look or status that shows who has what because of money. All our looks are earned (S10), and visitors see no counts (S6, S9).

### Punch-Out!! (Wii, Nintendo)

**Facts:** reviewers describe it as pattern recognition. Each opponent telegraphs attacks with visual and sound tells, and the player learns to dodge and counter at the right moment ([WIRED](https://www.wired.com/2009/05/punch-out-review/); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/punch-out-review-call-it-a-comeback-453114619); [Iwata Asks](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wii/punchout/0/4/)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** this is our Counter loop: telegraph, then Perfect Counter. Give each enemy a clear tell in both sight and sound (sound for accessibility).
- **Borrow:** a practice mode for each enemy fits our Monster Journal.

### Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice (FromSoftware)

**Facts:**

- Deflecting attacks fills the enemy's posture meter. When it breaks, a finishing blow opens up ([Activision support](https://support.activision.com/sekiro/articles/sekiro-shadows-die-twice-game-mechanics); [Polygon guide](https://www.polygon.com/sekiro-guide/2019/3/21/18274151/vitality-posture-damage-deflect-deathblows/)).
- There are no difficulty settings, and players called for an easy mode ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/sekiro-has-players-calling-for-an-easy-mode-has-fromsoftware-finally-gone-too-far-this-time/); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/sekiro-review/)).

**For us:**

- **Borrow:** good timing rewards you twice (safety plus progress). Our brute "winded" state works like a posture break.
- **Avoid:** a single fixed difficulty. Kids need Guided (S5).

#### The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Switch, 2019) and A Link Between Worlds (3DS, 2013)

- **Numbers:** Link's Awakening sold 6.46 million units by Dec 31, 2022 (CESA White Book, via [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/tons-of-sales-data-revealed-for-first-and-third-party-switch-3ds-games/)).
- **Why it worked (sources):** in A Link Between Worlds, Ravio's shop lets you rent most key items early, so most dungeons can be done in any order. Reviewers praised the freedom; some said it made each dungeon lean on one item ([Zelda Universe review](https://zeldauniverse.net/2013/11/21/review-the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds/), [Zelda Dungeon: Aonuma on Ravio's shop](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/aonuma-explains-how-ravios-shop-works/)).
- **Fits our rules:** a choice of which floor path to try next, with no fixed order inside a belt tier (guess: only within a tier, so belt gates stay).
- **Does not fit:** Link Between Worlds takes rented items back when you fall. We never take things away.

#### Moonlighter (Digital Sun, 2018)

- **Numbers:** over 1 million copies by May 2020, with the Switch the biggest revenue source, and 3.5 million total players ([11 bit studios press release via GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/archives/336053-pr-one-million-copies-of-moonlighter-sold-as-between-dimensions-expansion-launches)). Over 2 million copies by June 2022 ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/11-bit-studios-celebrates-2-million-copies-of-moonlighter-and-1-million-copies-of-children-of-morta-sold)).
- **Why it worked (sources):** it pairs dungeon runs with a calm shop and town to come home to. The studio credits its community and "numerous free updates" (same press release).
- **Fits our rules:** a calm home space between runs. We already have the dojo room (S7). Free content updates, never paid.
- **Does not fit:** thieves in the shop and losing items on a failed run. Nothing is taken away in our game.

#### Children of Morta (Dead Mage, 2019)

- **Numbers:** over 1 million copies by June 2022 ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/11-bit-studios-celebrates-2-million-copies-of-moonlighter-and-1-million-copies-of-children-of-morta-sold)).
- **Why it worked (sources):** you play six family members, each with a different style. "Family traits" mean that levelling one member gives a bonus to everyone, so no one is left weak ([Xbox Wire, by the developers](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2019/08/07/children-of-morta-preview-xbox-one/), [Game Developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/making-family-matter-in-i-children-of-morta-i-)). Reviewers liked the family scenes between runs ([GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/children-of-morta-review/1900-6417304/), [Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/review/children-of-morta/children-of-morta-review-family-matters)).
- **Fits our rules:** shared growth. A dojo-wide "everyone gets a little stronger" bonus from belt milestones. Classmates could show up to help in runs now and then (guess).
- **Does not fit:** "corruption fatigue" lowers a hero's health if you use them too much. That is a penalty, so we skip it.

#### Vampire Survivors, mobile (poncle, 2022)

- **Numbers:** no mobile sales or player figure found in a source I trust, so none is given.
- **Why it worked (sources):** the full game was free on phones, playable offline, with no microtransactions. The founder said: "I'm personally not a fan of microtransactions... This is the full game, for free, for real" ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/vampire-survivors-surprise-launches-to-mobile/)). Ads are optional (for a revive or gold after a run), and buying a DLC turns them off ([poncle support](https://poncle.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/14803391394705-How-do-I-buy-DLC-on-mobile)).
- **Fits our rules:** short runs, one-thumb play, offline play, the whole game with nothing to buy.
- **Does not fit:** ads of any kind, and paid DLC.

#### Kirby and the Forgotten Land (Nintendo, 2022)

- **Numbers:** 6.12 million by Dec 31, 2022 ([Nintendo Everything, CESA](https://nintendoeverything.com/tons-of-sales-data-revealed-for-first-and-third-party-switch-3ds-games/)). 7.52 million by March 31, 2024 ([Wikipedia, citing Nintendo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirby_and_the_Forgotten_Land)).
- **Why it worked (sources):** the accessibility report lists a choice of difficulty, helpful visual prompts and forgiving hits ([Family Gaming Database](https://www.familygamingdatabase.com/accessibility/Kirby+and+the+Forgotten+Land)). An accessibility writer writes about the hidden help Kirby gives players to make it more accessible ([Access-Ability](https://access-ability.uk/2022/04/25/lies-kirby-and-other-games-tell-you-to-improve-accessibility/)). Copy abilities can be upgraded, which gives a reason to replay ([Nintendo World Report review](http://www.nintendoworldreport.com/review/60126/kirby-and-the-forgotten-land-switch-review)).
- **Fits our rules:** a gentle mode with a kind name (our Steady, S5). Quiet help the kid doesn't have to ask for. Moves that grow when you use them.
- **Does not fit:** nothing big. Kirby is the closest match to our tone.

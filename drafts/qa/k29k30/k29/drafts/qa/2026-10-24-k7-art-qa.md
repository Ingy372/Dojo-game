- **WHAT:** I checked the 28 icons, the Sunrise Garden Tower (`?tower=t2`) in 7 room types including the boss room, and both auras (`?aura=swift`, `?aura=guarding`). I tested at 844x390 and 667x375 at phone sharpness (3x). There are 108 screenshots in `qa/k7/`.
- **WHY:** The new art has to look clear on a phone and must not hide the "!" warning, the danger circle, or the buttons.

**What I tested on.** The live link doesn't have K7 yet. Its new art files are missing (404) and its game code has no auras. So I ran a local copy of the latest main (`f89af8a`), using the developer server so the test could start rooms directly. I didn't change any game code.

## Bugs (most serious first)

**1. Gear text runs into itself on a 667-wide phone.**
- What I did: I opened Training Board → Gear with 8 items at 667x375.
- What happened: Names like "Leather Hand Wraps" and "Cloth Hand Wraps" wrap to two lines and sit on top of the stats line. Stats like "+14 Health +3 Guard" spill below the bottom of their box. Screenshot: `icons-667x375-gear.png`.
- What should have happened: Each card is easy to read with no overlap, like it looks at 844 (`icons-844x390-gear.png`). The new icon takes up about 46 px of width, and that pushes the text into wrapping.
- Severity: **ANNOYING**

**2. Technique Seal rows overlap on a 667-wide phone.**
- What I did: I opened Training Board → Abilities at 667x375.
- What happened: On the two Seal rows that have the new icon, the title wraps ("· Self-Defense #1" drops to a second line). It lands on top of the description and the "Sign off… to unlock" line. Screenshot: `icons-667x375-abilities.png`. The rows without an icon also run slightly tight, but the icon rows are the jumbled ones.
- What should have happened: The text stays readable. It's fine at 844 (`icons-844x390-abilities.png`).
- Severity: **ANNOYING**

**3. Insight icons are tiny inside their scroll.**
- What I did: I opened the "Choose an Insight" cards at 844x390.
- What happened: Each Insight icon is a wide scroll, and the picture on it (ripple, cup, leaf) is only about 10–12 px tall on screen. It's hard to tell them apart. Screenshot: `icons-insight-zoom.png`.
- What should have happened: The symbol is big enough to recognize at a glance. One option is to draw these at about 60 px, or have the Art Director crop the scroll tighter.
- Severity: **SMALL**

**4. The guide banner covers the boss's name (this was there before K7).**
- What I did: I opened `?tower=t2&room=keepers-hall`, bowed, and the Counter guide began.
- What happened: The Sensei banner sits over the "Floor Keeper" label under the boss health bar. Screenshot: `844x390-t2-swift-keepers-hall-*.png`.
- What should have happened: Both stay visible.
- Severity: **SMALL**

**5. Door labels sit under the health bar in the shrine and vault rooms (probably there before K7).**
- What I did: I opened `quiet-shrine` and `treasure-vault` at 667x375.
- What happened: The "BATTLE" and "CHALLENGE" door labels are partly behind the health bar and the "Room 1 of 8" text. Screenshots: `667x375-t2-quiet-shrine-*.png`, `667x375-t2-treasure-vault-*.png`.
- What should have happened: The labels are clear of the top bar.
- Severity: **SMALL**

## What passed
- **No missing art.** There were no 404s, load errors, or placeholder boxes in any run. Every icon id matches a real item, Insight, or Seal.
- **Sunrise Garden floors, walls, bamboo pillars, chest, and shrine all draw.** The light floor makes the dark-blue enemies and the player in the white gi stand out well.
- **The "!" warning and the red danger circle are as clear on the new floor as on the old one.** Side-by-side comparison: `warning-compare.png`. The "!" draws above the auras.
- **Auras.** Swift (gold streaks) and Guarding (blue dome) show on every enemy, animate, and draw nothing above the head. The enemy stays visible through both.
- **Speed.** Rooms opened in about 1.2–2.0 s, at about 60 frames per second. The worst single hitch was 83 ms, with 3 Guarding shields on screen at 667. One reading was 49 fps in the same room, but this computer has no graphics card, so it isn't a real phone result.

## How the art plays for each age
- **6-year-old:** The bright garden floor and green bamboo feel friendly. The red "!" still pops. The blue Guarding bubble looks like a "shield," which is good, because it will mean exactly that. The jumbled gear text (bug 1) would just confuse them.
- **12-year-old:** They'll like that a higher belt gets a new-looking tower. That's a clear reward for ranking up. They'll want the icons to be easy to tell apart at a glance (bug 3).
- **Adult:** The style is consistent and calm, with nothing scary. When 3 enemies with Guarding bubbles crowd the player, the screen gets busy. That's worth watching once elites are real.

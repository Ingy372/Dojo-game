# Tier 3: Orange Belt, The Ember Kitchens

**Belt form:** Keon  
**Guardian:** The Hearth Cook

## The Tower's history

Here the Tower cooked warm meals for the whole town. Everyone ate together at one long table.

## The shattered light

The ovens went cold. The long table was left empty, and the shadow liked the quiet.

## The Guardian: The Hearth Cook

A big, round cook with a ladle. The shadow made it guard the cold ovens alone.

- **When freed:** "Is it supper time? I would love some company."
- **Sensei, after:** "You kept going, one step at a time."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

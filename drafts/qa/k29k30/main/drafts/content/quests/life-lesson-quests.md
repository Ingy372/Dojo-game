# Life-lesson quests with choices (outlines)

"3rd belt" is read as the first three belt tiers: White, Yellow and Orange (see DECIDE). Two quests per tier, multi-part, each with real choices. Already drafted: "The Lost Wraps" (honesty, milestone-6.json), so honesty here takes a different angle. Themes from framework section 9: honesty, perseverance, handling anger, kindness, winning and losing gracefully, self-control. Nothing here ties to the Tower story's ending; Guardians are mentioned only as "a hard floor".

A weaker choice never loses Honor or anything else. It earns no Honor, the story goes somewhere less good (a sad face, a missed moment), Sensei reflects with a question, and the player can try that choice again.

## L1. The Empty Seat (Kindness)
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Include a new student who is left out.
- **Beats:** (1) A new kid sits alone by the wall. (2) Senpai's group is playing a pad game. (3) Player chooses. (4) Next day the new kid waves to the player first.
- **Choice point 1:** Invite them / keep playing / wave from far away.
  - Invite → they join, smile. Sensei: "One invite can change someone's whole day."
  - Keep playing → the kid looks down. Sensei: "How do you think they felt?" → choose again.
  - Wave → a small smile. Sensei: "A wave is a start. What could come next?" → choice 2.
- **Choice point 2:** Walk over and invite / leave it there.
- **Reward:** Honor 2; "Friendship Bench" decoration.
- **Learning inside:** Noticing others; kindness is an action.

## L2. Try Again Tomorrow (Perseverance)
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Keep going after not winning a hard floor.
- **Beats:** (1) Player loses on a hard floor (story scene, not real progress). (2) Back home, Kohai asks "Is that floor too hard?" (3) Player chooses. (4) Sensei shows the Training Board and the real practice the player does in class.
- **Choice point:** "I'll practice and try again." / "I quit." / "That floor is dumb."
  - Practice → Sensei: "Falling down is part of learning to stand."
  - Quit → Sensei: "That felt hard. Want to rest, then try?" → choose again (rest is allowed; quitting is reflected on, never shamed).
  - "Dumb" → Sensei: "It's okay to be frustrated. What would help?" → choose again.
- **Reward:** Honor 2; badge "Get Back Up".
- **Learning inside:** Mistakes are part of learning; real practice is how you get stronger. (Great Effort is a real stripe word; this links to it without granting it.)

## L3. The Teasing Rival (Handling anger)
- **Belt tier:** Yellow (2)
- **Goal:** Stay calm when someone teases before a challenge.
- **Beats:** (1) A rival kid from another dojo says "Your kicks are slow!" (2) Player feels the anger meter wobble (a visual, no numbers). (3) Breathing mini-game: tap slowly 3 times. (4) Player chooses. (5) At the end the rival says "Good match" or walks off grumpy, depending on the choice.
- **Choice point:** Take a breath and say "Let's have a good match." / tease back / walk away calmly.
  - Good match → Sensei: "Calm is a strong place to stand."
  - Tease back → rival gets louder. Sensei: "Did that make it better?" → choose again.
  - Walk away → Sensei: "Walking away can be brave too."
- **Reward:** Honor 2.
- **Learning inside:** Self-control; breathing to calm down; words can cool or heat things. (Self-Discipline is a real stripe word.)

## L4. Sorry, Kohai (Forgiveness)
- **Belt tier:** Yellow (2), matches the Yellow life word F = Forgiveness
- **Goal:** Forgive, and also say sorry when you're the one who made a mistake.
- **Beats:** (1) Part one: Kohai breaks the player's paper lantern by mistake and is scared to say so. (2) Player chooses. (3) Part two (next visit): the player accidentally bumps Senpai's water bottle over. (4) Player chooses how to make it right.
- **Choice point 1:** "It's okay. I forgive you." / "You ruined it!" / "Let's make a new one."
  - Forgive → Sensei: "Forgiving makes room for a friend."
  - Ruined → Kohai looks sad. Sensei: "It was an accident. What could help now?" → choose again.
  - New one → Sensei: "Forgiveness with helping hands."
- **Choice point 2:** "Sorry, Senpai! I'll clean it up." / pretend it didn't happen.
  - Sorry → Senpai: "Thanks for telling me!" Sensei: "Saying sorry is strong too."
  - Pretend → Senpai slips a bit (no harm). Sensei: "What could you say now?" → choose again.
- **Reward:** Honor 2; "Paper Lantern" decoration.
- **Learning inside:** Forgiving others and owning your own mistakes.

## L5. The Big Win (Winning gracefully / Humility)
- **Belt tier:** Orange (3)
- **Goal:** Win kindly and encourage others.
- **Beats:** (1) Player wins a friendly target-kick contest at the dojo. (2) Kohai finished last and looks sad. (3) Player chooses. (4) Kohai asks for a tip.
- **Choice point:** "Great job, Kohai! Want to practice together?" / "I'm the best!" / bow to everyone.
  - Encourage → Sensei: "Lifting others up is a winner's best move."
  - "Best" → Kohai walks away. Sensei: "How did Kohai look just now?" → choose again.
  - Bow → Sensei: "A bow says thank you to everyone who played."
- **Reward:** Honor 2; title "Good Sport".
- **Learning inside:** Humility; winning and losing gracefully.

## L6. Room for Everyone (Acceptance)
- **Belt tier:** Orange (3), matches the Orange life word A = Acceptance
- **Goal:** Accept someone who is different.
- **Beats:** (1) A new student kicks in an unusual way (they learned somewhere else). (2) Some kids giggle. (3) Player chooses. (4) Later the new student shows the player a cool stretch.
- **Choice point:** "That's different. Can you show me?" / giggle along / say nothing.
  - Ask to learn → Sensei: "Different can be a gift."
  - Giggle → the new kid's face falls. Sensei: "How would that feel if it were you?" → choose again.
  - Nothing → Sensei: "You didn't laugh. What could you add?" → choose again.
- **Reward:** Honor 2; badge "Room for Everyone".
- **Learning inside:** Acceptance; curiosity instead of teasing.

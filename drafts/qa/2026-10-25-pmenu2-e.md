1. **Hint is missing on small phones.** What I did: opened Fighting Style at 667×375. What happened: "Tap a card, then tap a pick." is dropped because the preview card is too short. Should have happened: the one how-to line shows on the smallest phone too, for example by shortening the switch line or the body. ANNOYING.
2. **Locked tiles don't say what they are.** What I did: opened the Virtue tray. What happened: both locked tiles say only "Learn this at the dojo", and the name appears only after tapping. Should have happened: show the name, with "Learn this at the dojo" under it or in the sheet. ANNOYING.
3. **A perk has no icon.** What I did: looked at "Always with you" for the green belt. What happened: one item is a plain letter "F" in a box, among picture icons. Should have happened: a real icon. SMALL.
4. **Re-tapping the equipped pick re-saves and pops.** What I did: tapped the tile that was already equipped. What happened: equipPick always returns a new object, so the save is rewritten and the card pops again. No harm done. Should have happened: nothing. SMALL. The locked-sheet icon is also an empty circle: SMALL.

## Issues (most serious first)
**1. Picking a card can make that card vanish (ANNOYING, pre-existing, not new in #253)**
- **What I did:** On the Gear tab with a full bag, I tapped a card in the bottom row.
- **What happened:** The detail box opens and takes space, so a row is dropped. At 667, the grid goes from 4 cards to 2. At 844 it goes from 6 to 4. The card I tapped is no longer on screen, and the page count jumps (1/8 to 1/15 at 667). See `rmoff-667-selected.png`.
- **Should have happened:** The card you tapped stays visible and highlighted, and the cards don't move. For example, keep the grid fixed and show the detail on top of it, or keep the selected item on the page.

**2. Gear item cards still use plain taps (SMALL, left over from P-MENU-a issue 3)**
- **What I did:** I held a finger on a card for 110 ms. I also pressed off the card and slid onto it before lifting.
- **What happened:** The card scale stayed 1, there's no tap sound, and the corners are square. Sliding onto a card and lifting still selects it.
- **Should have happened:** It should work like the #228 buttons: rounded, press to 0.96, soft tap, and slide-on does nothing.

**3. At 667, the "Now wearing" line is cut off (SMALL, pre-existing)**
- **What happened:** With a long worn name, "Now wearing: Grandmaster Thunder Tiger Stripe Hand Wraps (…)" wraps to a second line that is hidden behind the Wear and Let go buttons.
- **Should have happened:** Fit it on one line, shortened with "…".

## Playability
- **6-year-old:** The tab slide is smooth and calm. Tapping a card and seeing it disappear is confusing ("where did my thing go?"). Eight pages of 4 cards each at 667 is a lot of paging for a small child.
- **12-year-old:** Tabs feel snappy. They will notice that cards don't squish like the other buttons.
- **Adult/parent:** No pressure text. Reduced motion is honest (fade only). Let go has a safe "Sure?" step.

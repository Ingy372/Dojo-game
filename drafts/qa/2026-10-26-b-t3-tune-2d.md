# B-T3-TUNE-2d: tier 3 (Orange) boss replay on main 508652c2 (QA, Mon Oct 26, 2026, ET)

## Delivery Note
- ITEM: B-T3-TUNE-2d. Tier 3 Orange boss (great-hearth, hearth-cook) on Guided, Standard and Challenge.
- WHAT: PASS on the core targets. A typical student wins 100% in about 15 s, the 6-year-old wins far above 75%, no cheap deaths, and lower gear still loses. Four DECIDE items are below.
- WHY: The sim (60 seeds × 4 bot styles × 3 difficulties, plus Green and Yellow gear for comparison) and 8 real-touch browser fights at 667x375 agree.
- DECIDE:
  1. **Browser gear mismatch.** In the browser, tier 3 (3-yellow-belt plus beltEntry Orange stripe 2) still plays with the profile's own stats: 195 HP, power 24, guard 25. The sim's Orange gear is 332.79 / 45.85 / 33.53. There is no Orange fixture on the hidden testing screen. A real Orange student's stats depend on their training feed. Which numbers are the truth for tuning?
  2. **Newer student HP loss is too low.** Standard averages 6% (worst 18%), against a 15–25% target (about half at worst). Bots counter more cleanly than real newer students, so this is the known bot caveat. It may be fine in real life, or the boss may be a bit soft for a newer student.
  3. **Challenge is too easy.** The 6-year-old wins 59 of 60 and the masher 53 of 60 (88–98%), against a target of "about 2 in 3".
  4. **Guided plays the same as Standard.** For the masher: 58/60 on Guided vs 59/60 on Standard, 23% vs 21% HP lost. The kid takes 20.0 s on Guided vs 18.8 s on Standard. Guided gives no measurable help at Orange. SMALL.
- NEXT: The Manager decides 1–4. If tuning is wanted, the Programmer changes the Challenge levers (and maybe Guided), then QA re-runs this exact sim table.

## How it feels
- **6-year-old:** wins almost every time even while mashing. About 19–20 s, sometimes ends at half HP, which is exciting but safe. Good. On Challenge it is still a near-certain win, so it isn't a challenge.
- **12-year-old:** about 15 s, rarely hit. It may feel a bit easy. Challenge feels about the same as Standard.
- **Adult:** a short, readable fight. Challenge is not demanding at Orange gear.
- Training matters: at Green gear, the same boss takes 20 s and the kid loses about 1 in 6 on Standard. At Yellow, a newer student loses 1 in 9 on Standard and the kid can't reach it.

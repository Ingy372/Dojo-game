# QA report: live build "Version: Oct 18, 7:45 AM" (commit 586641d)

Tested 2026-10-18 in Chrome DevTools phone emulation (Pixel 9 Pro, landscape, touch). The emulator lags input, so movement feel, Perfect Counter timing, and a full run still need a real phone. The browser had an existing save (Sam, Yellow Belt), so true first-time creation and first-run tutorial were not seen.

## Bug reports (most serious first; paste into Claude Code)

### 1. Standard and Guided room 1 is too punishing, and a loss has no message — ANNOYING
- What I did: entered the Tower and pressed Strike repeatedly in Room 1 (Oct 12 on `?difficulty=guided`, Oct 18 on default Standard).
- What happened: the run ended in about 14 seconds at Wave 2 of 3 both times. On Oct 18 the game jumped straight to the Training Board ("You reached room 1 of 8 · Time 0:14", "+0 experience") with no defeat screen.
- What should have happened: Guided should let a 6-year-old who mashes Strike get through Room 1. Every loss should show a short, encouraging screen with a clear "Try again" button. (The audit in drafts/learning/audit-m0-4.md reports a 99% win rate for the simulated novice on Guided, which does not match real play.)

### 2. Nothing teaches Counter or the "!" warning — ANNOYING
- What I did: played Room 1 on Oct 12 and Oct 18.
- What happened: the only help is the button labels (Virtue, Dash, Strike, Counter, Form). Nothing explains what "!" means or when to press Counter.
- What should have happened: a guided first Counter, as drafted in drafts/content/tutorial/sensei-lessons.json (bot-content, 40c272b) and Proposal 6 in drafts/learning/proposals-top5.md (bot-learning, 2d0c36b).

### 3. The promotion sequence is too many screens — ANNOYING
- What I did: switched to the Yellow Belt profile and tapped through the ceremony.
- What happened: 8 tap-to-continue screens in a row (Promotion, Gate opens, New at Yellow Belt, Promotion rewards, Character stripe x2, 13 techniques signed off, Sensei's Seal), then the Welcome-back popup.
- What should have happened: 2 or 3 screens at most, with the rest viewable later from the Board.

### 4. Jargon on the ceremony screens — ANNOYING
- What I did: read the promotion screens.
- What happened: they use terms a 6-year-old won't know yet: "Technique Seal", "Form", "Strike upgrade", "Self-Defense #6", "Catch-up from White Belt".
- What should have happened: plain words, or new terms only once they've been taught.

### 5. Bow prompt at every dojo entry and every floor — SMALL
- What I did: reloaded the page and entered floors.
- What happened: "Bow as you enter your dojo" shows after every reload, and "Bow to begin the floor" shows at the start of every floor.
- What should have happened: confirm this is intended. If it is, make it one quick tap.

### 6. "Dust settled while you were away" may read as guilt — SMALL
- What I did: came back to the Home Dojo.
- What happened: "A little dust settled while you were away." plus "Mess: 81% (97 hours built up)".
- What should have happened: frame it positively (for example, "Your dojo is ready for a fresh sweep!") and drop the hours counter.

### 7. Desktop clicks don't start the game — SMALL for now
- What I did: clicked "Tap to start" with a mouse.
- What happened: nothing happens, because `main.ts` sets `input: { mouse: false }`.
- What should have happened: fine if mobile-only is intended. A desktop or browser preview would need mouse input enabled.

### 8. Testing-screen docs mismatch — SMALL (docs)
- Tapping Home Dojo 5 times opens "Dojo testing". Profiles and Power Rating are behind tapping the Training Board title 5 times. Update docs/GROK_BOTS.md, or the game, so they match.

### 9. Character creation details — SMALL
- The Boy/Girl toggle and Eyes 1–6 barely change the preview at phone size.
- The 10-swatch colour rows are small to tap.
- The button names change: "Let's go!" the first time, then "Look", "Done", and "Back", and "Back" doesn't say whether it saves.
- Not checked: whether Back discards unsaved edits.

### 10. Tidy-up counter is unexplained — SMALL
- "Tidy up: 20 left" has no explanation. The camera also zooms and shifts when Decorate opens.

## Fixed or passing
- Stick knob is now drawn and follows the thumb.
- Progress, look, belt, and name survive a reload, and the ceremony doesn't repeat.
- Character creation works, and the look shows in the ceremony, the Home Dojo, and the Board.
- Decorating is clear: a move-style choice, step prompts, and a "find more in Tower chests" hint.
- Design rules: Power Rating appears only on the hidden testing screen. Nothing for sale, no chat. Death text from Oct 12 was gentle.

## Not tested yet (needs a real phone)
- A full Standard run and its time (target 5–7 minutes).
- Boss door, golden "set free" effect, Floor Keeper, Insights, loot, and the run summary.
- Perfect Counter feel.
- Tower door taps.
- Guided mode on the Oct 18 build.
- Full dojo cleaning.

## Playability
- Most fun: the promotion confetti with your own character, the combo and hit numbers, and moving the rug.
- Most boring: tapping through the ceremony screens.
- Load for a 6-year-old: too many ceremony screens, five combat buttons with no teaching, and small swatches.
- Coming back: decorations from Tower chests are a good, pressure-free reason to return. The dust-while-away wording is the one place it could tip into pressure.

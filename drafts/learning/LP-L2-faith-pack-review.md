## 1. Full KJV or wall wording: recommend the wall wording

**Recommendation: show the wall wording (`showText: wall`) for all 10 verses.** The pack's `wallText` already has the two sign typos fixed ("come short", "died").

Why, for 6–9s:
1. **It matches what they see in class.** Kids learn a verse from the dojo wall and from saying it together. The game helps most when it repeats exactly those words, so in-game reading and class practice strengthen one memory instead of two competing versions. Practice transfers best when the practice matches the real test (transfer-appropriate processing: Morris, Bransford & Franks, 1977, *Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior* 16, 519–533). **MODERATE**: a well-replicated idea, but studied with adults, not with scripture or children.
2. **It's easier to read.** The KJV adds words a young reader can't decode or doesn't know: "believeth", "seeth", "thee", "Verily, verily", "Except a man…", "sent not". The wall keeps the KJV feel ("begotten", "commendeth") with fewer stumbling points. Harder, unfamiliar words slow young readers and cost understanding. **WEAK as a formal claim here**: it's common sense and general reading research, and there's no study of these exact texts.
3. **John 3:3 and 3:5 differ most** (KJV starts "Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily…"). Those are brown-belt verses, so the readers are usually older, but the wall still matches class better.

The tension: section 6 of `docs/faith-and-creed.md` says Jay chose KJV "the same as on the walls". The pack shows that the walls are not the KJV on 9 of 10 verses. So Jay's two reasons (KJV, and matching the walls) point different ways. Only Jay can pick. If he wants KJV for faith reasons, the game should use KJV, and the learning cost is small for the early belts (John 3:16, Romans 3:10, 3:23 change only a little).

Everything else **passes**:

- The life-word meanings are short and concrete. "Servanthood", "Integrity" and "Acceptance" are hard words, but they're the real dojo words, so keep them and let voice help.

- The Sensei questions point to real life and other people, not to the game. No faith item is a lock or a quiz, which matches section 6, answer 5.

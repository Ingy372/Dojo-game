## stances.json: small changes (Content Designer's call)

1. **Open Leg `description`** "The open leg stance." just repeats the name. Use the how-to-stand line Jay gave instead.
2. **Cat `description`** "It shows up in every kata so far." is staff wording, and "so far" will go stale. Suggestion: "A short step back."
3. **Horse `description`** "A rooted stance." Young kids may not know "rooted." Suggestion: "Stand strong and still." This matches its game line. How to actually stand in Horse is still Jay's to answer. This only describes the feeling.
4. **Beginner's `gameLine`** has 11 words in one sentence. If it gets touched again, split it: "Everyone starts here." / "Your teacher can teach you stances." It's already approved and live, so this is optional.
5. **The four empty `howToStand` lines** (Cat, Front, Fighting, Horse) stay empty until Jay answers. Don't guess them. When they come in: one sentence of 8 words or fewer, feet first, then hands, and ideally next to the stance icon.

Unlocking is already paced well. Each stance opens only after a teacher's sign-off, so a white belt meets them one at a time, not all six at once. The "but" in each game line (for example, "Hit harder, but move a little slower") teaches a real trade-off in one line. Keep that.

## For when the kata steps arrive

- **Never show the sheet text to kids.** Lines like "Step forward w/RF, pivot on the balls of feet…" are written for instructors. Kids should see the move name from `moves.json`, the icon, and a facing arrow, one step at a time.
- **One step per screen**, with the kiai marked by the existing Kiai icon. Breaking a sequence into small, learner-paced pieces helps learning (Rey et al., 2019, meta-analysis of the segmenting effect, *Educational Psychology Review* 31, 389–419). **MODERATE**: the evidence is strong overall, but mostly from older students, not 6-year-olds.
- **The game reminds, class teaches.** Keep the shared `senseiLine` "Practice your kata with your instructor." on every kata. No kata screen should feel like a test.
- **Character lines** (for example "slow and strong") should be 5 words or fewer, from Jay. Don't invent them.

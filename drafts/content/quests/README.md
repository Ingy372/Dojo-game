# C4 part 1: quest outlines (manners, culture, life lessons)

OUTLINES ONLY. Full scripts come after Jay approves. No main story chapters here, and nothing depends on the Tower story's ending.

- `manners-quests.md`: 5 Dojo manners quests
- `culture-quests.md`: 4 culture quests
- `life-lesson-quests.md`: 6 life-lesson quests with choices, 2 each for White, Yellow and Orange
- `quests-skeleton.json`: the same 15 quests as a skeleton in the quest format already used by `drafts/content/dialogue/milestone-6.json` (`id`, `kind`, `name`, `trigger`, `honor`, `lines`, `branches`). `content/` has no quest data format yet, so this follows the draft dialogue format.

Rules followed: ages 6 to 9, one short sentence per screen, hopeful. Sensei reflects and never lectures. A weaker choice never loses Honor; it earns none, the story goes somewhere less good, and Sensei helps the player think it through (framework section 9). No purchases, ads, chat, timers, streaks, guilt or pressure. Real training stays the main source of power: rewards are Honor, decorations, badges and titles, never combat power. Characters use roles only (Sensei, Senpai, Kohai, neighbors); no real student data. Already-drafted quests (`quest-manners-bow` "The Proper Bow" and `quest-life-honesty` "The Lost Wraps") are not repeated.

Reward amounts are placeholders (Honor 1 for manners and culture, 2 for life lessons, matching milestone-6.json).

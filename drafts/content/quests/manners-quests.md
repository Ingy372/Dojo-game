# Dojo manners quests (outlines)

Source: framework section 9 (bowing, greeting Sensei, lining up by rank, caring for equipment, addressing others respectfully). The bow at the door is already "The Proper Bow" in milestone-6.json and the everyday bow ritual, so it isn't repeated.

## M1. Good Morning, Sensei
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Greet Sensei properly when arriving.
- **Beats:** Player arrives at the Home Dojo. Sensei is sweeping. Kohai runs past without a word. Player chooses a greeting. Kohai watches and copies.
- **Choice point:** "Good morning, Sensei!" with a bow / a quick wave / say nothing.
  - Sensei (bow): "A warm hello starts a good day."
  - Sensei (wave): "Hello! What else could we say with a bow?" → replay the choice.
  - Sensei (nothing): "I'm glad you're here. Hello!" → replay the choice.
- **Reward:** Honor 1; a "Welcome Mat" decoration.
- **Learning inside:** Greeting the teacher by title, with a bow, is respect.

## M2. Line Up!
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Line up by rank before class.
- **Beats:** Sensei claps once. Senpai, Kohai and the player find spots. Player taps where to stand. Class bows in together.
- **Choice point:** Stand by rank / rush to the front / stand next to a friend.
  - Sensei (by rank): "Everyone has a place. Yours is right here."
  - Sensei (front): "Front is the higher belts' spot. Where's yours?" → retry.
  - Sensei (friend): "Friends are great. Where does your belt stand?" → retry.
- **Reward:** Honor 1.
- **Learning inside:** Lining up by rank, and that every rank has a place.

## M3. Back on the Rack
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Put training gear away after practice.
- **Beats:** After a Training Board session, pads and targets are on the floor. Kohai trips a little (no injury, just a wobble). Player chooses. Player drags gear to the rack (simple sort).
- **Choice point:** Put it all away / leave it for later / help Kohai put theirs away too.
  - Sensei (put away): "A tidy dojo keeps everyone safe."
  - Sensei (later): "Hmm. What happened when Kohai walked by?" → retry.
  - Sensei (help Kohai too): "Two helpers, half the time!"
- **Reward:** Honor 1; a "Gear Rack" decoration.
- **Learning inside:** Caring for equipment; tidiness keeps others safe.

## M4. Yes, Sensei
- **Belt tier:** Yellow (2)
- **Goal:** Answer Sensei respectfully and listen.
- **Beats:** Sensei gives a simple instruction ("Three steps, then stop."). Player answers. Player follows it in a short movement mini-game.
- **Choice point:** "Yes, Sensei!" / "Okay." / "Why?"
  - Sensei ("Yes, Sensei!"): "Clear and ready. I can hear you're listening."
  - Sensei ("Okay."): "I hear you. How do we answer in the dojo?" → retry.
  - Sensei ("Why?"): "Good question! Let's try it, then ask." (a curious question is never wrong; player then answers "Yes, Sensei!")
- **Reward:** Honor 1.
- **Learning inside:** Addressing the teacher by title and listening first.

## M5. Senpai's Help
- **Belt tier:** Yellow (2)
- **Goal:** Thank an older student who helps you.
- **Beats:** Player's belt knot comes loose. Senpai shows how to tie it. Player responds.
- **Choice point:** Bow and say "Thank you, Senpai!" / say nothing and run off / say "I can do it myself!"
  - Sensei (thank): "Thank you is a small word that does big things."
  - Sensei (run off): "Senpai helped. What could you tell them?" → retry.
  - Sensei (myself): "Trying is good. Help is good too." → retry.
- **Reward:** Honor 1.
- **Learning inside:** Respect for senior students; accepting help gracefully.

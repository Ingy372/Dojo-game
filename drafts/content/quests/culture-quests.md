# Culture quests (outlines)

Source: framework section 9 (school terms, belt meanings, arts blended in the style, founding story) and docs/faith-and-creed.md. Culture content comes from Action Zone's content pack; anything the docs don't give is marked as a placeholder for Jay.

## K1. Dojo Words
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Learn what Sensei, Senpai and Kohai mean.
- **Beats:** Kohai is confused by the names. Player meets each character. A matching game pairs each word with its meaning ("teacher", "older student", "newer student").
- **Choice point:** When Kohai asks "Am I a Kohai forever?": "One day you'll be a Senpai!" / "Yes, forever." / "Let's ask Sensei."
  - Sensei (Senpai one day): "Every Senpai was a Kohai once."
  - Sensei (forever): "Hmm. Who was Senpai before?" → retry.
  - Sensei (ask): "Great idea. Every Senpai was a Kohai once."
- **Reward:** Honor 1; badge "Dojo Words".
- **Learning inside:** The school's terms, and that everyone grows.

## K2. Colors of the Climb
- **Belt tier:** White (1)
- **Goal:** Learn the belt order, White to Black.
- **Beats:** Senpai shows a wall of belts. Player puts the belts in order (White, Yellow, Orange, Green, Blue, Red, Red/Black, Brown, Brown/Black, Black from docs/curriculum.md). Sensei shows where the player's belt sits.
- **Choice point:** "Which belt is the most important?": "Black, of course!" / "The one I'm wearing!" / "All of them."
  - Sensei (Black): "Every black belt started in white."
  - Sensei (mine): "Yes! Your belt is where you are right now."
  - Sensei (all): "Every step matters. Even the first."
- **Reward:** Honor 1; "Belt Wall" decoration.
- **Learning inside:** The belt order; every rank matters. (Belt colour meanings are a placeholder; see DECIDE.)

## K3. Jesus' Way
- **Belt tier:** Yellow (2)
- **Goal:** Learn that Yeshua's Ryu means "Jesus' Way".
- **Beats:** Kohai asks what the name on the dojo sign means. Sensei explains in one line. Player visits the Creed Wall and hears the class creed's first line read by the computer voice (wording from creed-and-verses.json).
- **Choice point:** None that grades. Player picks what to ask next: "Why 'Jesus' Way'?" / "What's a creed?"; both lead to a warm answer.
  - Sensei: "We train our bodies, and we grow our hearts."
- **Reward:** Honor 1; badge "Jesus' Way".
- **Learning inside:** The school's name and that faith is part of its story. Faith content is welcome (Jay), kept light.

## K4. Letters on the Wall (FAITH DOES)
- **Belt tier:** Yellow (2), first at the first promotion
- **Goal:** Discover that each belt promotion adds one life word, spelling FAITH DOES.
- **Beats:** After the White → Yellow promotion, a glowing "F" appears on the Creed Wall. Sensei says it stands for Forgiveness. Kohai notices the empty spaces and wonders what comes next.
- **Choice point:** Kohai has accidentally knocked over the player's decoration. "It's okay, I forgive you." / "Be more careful!" / "Let's fix it together."
  - Sensei (forgive): "That's F, right there."
  - Sensei (careful): "Kohai feels bad. What could help?" → retry.
  - Sensei (fix together): "Forgiveness with helping hands!"
- **Reward:** Honor 1. The letter itself comes from the real promotion, not from this quest.
- **Learning inside:** FAITH DOES life words (docs/faith-and-creed.md section 5); the first word, Forgiveness, practiced.

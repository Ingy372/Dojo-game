# Dojo Ascent Lore Bible (C3 draft)

One page per belt tier, White to Black. Every quest and enemy (C4) builds on this. Draft until Jay approves.

## The story in one breath

Long ago, God gave the town a great light. It shone at the top of the Tower and kept everyone warm and safe. One night, a frightened student tried to keep the light all to themselves. They held it so tightly that it broke into ten pieces. Shadow slipped into the empty spaces, floor by floor. Each piece fell to one tier, and that tier's Guardian now holds it, confused by the shadow. Every real belt the player earns brings one piece home. At the summit, the player finds the student who broke it and brings them home too. The light was a gift, and gifts are meant to be shared.

## Rules this lore keeps

- **Real training is the power.** Beating a Guardian gives a spark and a trophy. The light only returns when the real belt opens the Gate (content decisions #2).
- **Nothing is evil forever.** Every Guardian and monster is a Tower thing the shadow changed, and beating it frees it (content decisions #4).
- **The mystery unfolds by Relics.** Each tier has 3 Relics with one clue each. The full answer arrives only at black belt.
- **Faith is gentle.** The light is God's gift. Verses are only cited by reference here; their wording comes from `faith/creed-and-verses.json` and is never reworded.
- **Sensei reflects and never lectures.** One short sentence per Sensei line.

## Faith thread

The verse that goes with each promotion is in `docs/faith-and-creed.md` (John 3:16 at White to Yellow, through John 6:40 at Black Belt). The lore echoes the same path: a good gift, everyone falls short, forgiveness, coming home. The summit ending mirrors the creed's last line, "More Than a Conqueror": the player wins by bringing someone home, not by defeating them.

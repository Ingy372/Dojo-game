# Tier 2: Yellow Belt, The Sunrise Garden

**Belt form:** Basic Form 2  
**Guardian:** The Sun Gardener

## The Tower's history

Above the halls grew a garden that greeted the sun each morning. Statues and little lights kept it safe.

## The shattered light

The shadow made the garden sleepy and confused. Old Thumper, Zippy Zoom and Captain Clank forgot their friends.

## The Guardian: The Sun Gardener

A tall, gentle gardener made of vines. The shadow wrapped it in thorns, but its roots still reach for the sun.

- **When freed:** "The thorns are gone. My flowers can open again."
- **Sensei, after:** "You moved light on your feet. The garden noticed."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

The pieces of light fell in a circle around the Tower. Something was standing in the middle.

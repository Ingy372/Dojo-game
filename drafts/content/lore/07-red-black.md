# Tier 7: Red/Black Belt, The Hall of Echoes

**Belt form:** Penon 3  
**Guardian:** The Echo Mask

## The Tower's history

This hall repeats whatever you say. Teachers used it to speak words of courage to the whole Tower.

## The shattered light

Now it only echoes unkind words and fears. The shadow loves to repeat them.

## The Guardian: The Echo Mask

A floating mask that copies your moves. Under the mask, it is lonely.

- **When freed:** "I only copied what I heard. I would like to hear kind words now."
- **Sensei, after:** "You chose your own words, not the echo's."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

## Relics

| Relic | What the player reads | Sensei |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traveler's Note | "If I keep the light, the dark can never reach me." | Light grows when it is shared, not hidden. |
| Kind Word Stone | When you whisper to it, it whispers back: "You are loved." | You are loved. |
| Half Mask | A mask cut in two. One side smiles. | Be the same person inside and out. |

## What the player learns

The traveler heard the echoes too. They began to believe the light was not safe with anyone else.

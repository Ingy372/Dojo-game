# Tier 9: Brown/Black Belt, The Storm Stair

**Belt form:** Possi  
**Guardian:** The Storm Herald

## The Tower's history

Wind and rain once washed this stair clean each spring. The Tower felt fresh and new.

## The shattered light

Now a storm spins without stopping. It hides the summit in thick clouds.

## The Guardian: The Storm Herald

A tall figure of wind and lightning. It shouts the storm louder so no one hears the calm.

- **When freed:** "The wind is still. Listen. Someone is calling from the top."
- **Sensei, after:** "You stayed steady inside the storm."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

## Relics

| Relic | What the player reads | Sensei |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm Feather | It floats still, even in the strongest wind. | Peace can be inside you, even in a storm. |
| Unsent Letter | "I'm sorry. I broke it. I'm too afraid to come down." | No one is too far to come home. |
| Rainbow Shard | A piece of the great light, shining every color. | After the storm comes a promise. |

## What the player learns

The storm is the traveler's fear, grown very big. The traveler is at the summit, holding the last of the light.

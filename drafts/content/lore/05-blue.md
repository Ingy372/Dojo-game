# Tier 5: Blue Belt, The Quiet River

**Belt form:** Pinon 1  
**Guardian:** The River Watcher

## The Tower's history

A river once ran through the middle of the Tower. Boats carried light from floor to floor.

## The shattered light

The river grew still and dark. The boats drifted, and no one could see the far shore.

## The Guardian: The River Watcher

A heron made of water. The shadow muddied its eyes, so it cannot see who is friend or foe.

- **When freed:** "The water is clear. I can see you now, friend."
- **Sensei, after:** "You stayed calm in deep water."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

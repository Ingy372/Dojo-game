# Tier 4: Green Belt, The Giant's Steps

**Belt form:** David and Goliath  
**Guardian:** The Stone Giant

## The Tower's history

Huge stone steps climb this tier. Long ago, small helpers carried water up them every day.

## The shattered light

The shadow piled up into a giant made of stone and worry. It blocks the steps and looks very big.

## The Guardian: The Stone Giant

Very tall and very loud. Inside, it is only a pile of stones the shadow stacked up.

- **When freed:** "I was only stones and worry. Thank you for not running away."
- **Sensei, after:** "Big things can still fall to a brave, small heart."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

## Relics

| Relic | What the player reads | Sensei |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smooth River Stone | A small, smooth stone. It fits just right in your hand. | Small and ready is enough. |
| Little Water Jar | A helper's jar, still half full. They kept carrying water after dark. | Faithful work matters, even when no one sees. |
| Pebble of Worry | It looks heavy. It is light as air when you pick it up. | Some worries are smaller than they look. |

## What the player learns

The shadow is not one monster. It is made of many little fears, all stacked together.

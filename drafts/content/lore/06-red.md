# Tier 6: Red Belt, The Forge of Courage

**Belt form:** Penon 2  
**Guardian:** The Ember Knight

## The Tower's history

Smiths once made shields here for anyone who needed protecting. They sang while they worked.

## The shattered light

The fires burned too hot and wild. The smiths ran, and the forge forgot its song.

## The Guardian: The Ember Knight

A knight of glowing metal. The shadow told it to protect the forge from everyone.

- **When freed:** "I was guarding the wrong thing. Shields are for helping."
- **Sensei, after:** "Strength is best used to protect others."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

## What the player learns

The traveler who took the light wore red. Their shield was found here, cracked.

# Tier 1: White Belt, The Lantern Halls

**Belt form:** Basic Form 1  
**Guardian:** The Lamp Keeper (already in `story/chapter-1.json`)

## The Tower's history

The first floors were built for beginners. Lanterns lined every hall. A kind keeper lit them each night.

## The shattered light

When the light broke, the lanterns went out one by one. The keeper kept waiting for morning.

## The Guardian: The Lamp Keeper

Once it kept the Tower's first lamp lit. The shadow made it forget, but it never stopped hoping.

- **When freed:** "A spark... I had forgotten how warm it feels."
- **Sensei, after:** "Watch, wait, then answer. You did just that."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

## Relics

## What the player learns

The light was not lost. It broke into pieces, and the pieces are still here.

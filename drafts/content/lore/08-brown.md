# Tier 8: Brown Belt, The Root Library

**Belt form:** Nihanchi  
**Guardian:** The Root Keeper

## The Tower's history

Old tree roots hold up this library. Every book tells how someone was helped on their climb.

## The shattered light

The shadow covered the books in dust. Without the stories, climbers felt alone.

## The Guardian: The Root Keeper

A wise old tree who reads every book. The shadow made it forget how to share them.

- **When freed:** "Take a book, little one. Your story belongs here too."
- **Sensei, after:** "Strong roots keep you standing."
- **The light piece** rises only when the real belt opens the Gate.

## Relics

| Relic | What the player reads | Sensei |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dusty Story Book | A book of helpers. The last page is blank, waiting for a new story. | Your story is still being written. |
| Faded Student Sash | An old practice sash with a name too faded to read. | Every master was once a beginner. |
| Root Ring | A ring of wood that grows a little each time you read. | Learning grows deep before it grows tall. |

## What the player learns

The last book has a page about the traveler. It says they were once a student of this very Tower.

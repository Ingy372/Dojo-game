# Content decisions (Jay gave creative freedom, 2026-10-12)

1. **Floor Keeper is not the Guardian.** The Floor Keeper is the boss at the top of each floor (it's in the game now). Each tier's Guardian is a separate, bigger boss above floor 5. Framework and core-design describe the Guardian floor and the Gate that way. The white-tier Guardian is **The Lamp Keeper**. Floor Keeper lore ("kept the lamps lit on each floor") makes them the Lamp Keeper's helpers, so the story stays connected. (This replaces the earlier rename idea Floor Keeper → Lamp Keeper.)
2. **Light only returns with a real promotion.** Beating the Guardian gives a spark and a few words of dialogue. The fragment of light rises only when the Gate opens with the real belt. This follows framework: "Defeating the Guardian ... never opens the Gate."
3. **Material tiers:** bamboo and stone = white (simple building things), silk = yellow, jade = orange (rarest-looking, matches the Jade Charm being a strong charm). One new material per tier after white.
4. **Enemy story:** all enemies are made of shadow from the broken light, or are Tower things the shadow changed (statues → Brutes, door guards → Shield Guards). Nothing is evil forever. Keepers can be freed, which fits the hopeful tone.
5. **Faith:** gentle and hopeful. The light is God's gift. Its return is tied to Yeshua (John 1:5 echo), with 2 lines in chapter 1 and one Virtue reply. No fear, no threats, no preaching.
6. **Technique Callouts** use only exact data names (Basic Form 1/2, Kick Combo 1/2, Self-Defense #1-#10, and the stripe words). Orange has no abilities in abilities.json yet, so it has no callouts.
7. **Sensei's Question at the Bow** is recall (Jay, K1; C2). Wrong answers warmly show the right name, with no penalty. It can always be skipped.
8. New fields `description` and `bestiary` were approved by Jay.
